OpenWorld, Inc. Reports 2026 Third Quarter Results; First Quarterly Results as a Nasdaq-Listed Company

 

Q3 Marked Pivotal Shift to High-Margin RWA Platform

and Major Deal Origination Business

 

·Real-world assets (“RWA”) revenue increased to approximately $6 million in Q3 2026 from zero in Q3 2025

·Achieved Gross margins of approximately 80%

·Secured $1.1 billion in non-binding letter of intent (“LOI”) signings subsequent to Q3 Quarter-end with definitive agreements and DeFi capital formation now underway

 

LAKE MARY, Fla., November 12, 2026 — OpenWorld, Inc. (NASDAQ: OPNW) (“OpenWorld” or the “Company”), a technology-powered digital assets and blockchain innovation company focused on real-world asset (“RWA”) tokenization, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. The third quarter financial results presented in this press release primarily reflect the operating results of legacy OpenWorld, the accounting acquirer in the reverse takeover transaction with VerifyMe, Inc. (“VerifyMe”) completed on September 30, 2026. The Company's third quarter operating results do not include VerifyMe’s pre-transaction operating results, while the September 30, 2026 consolidated balance sheet reflects the combined entity.

 

2026 Third Quarter and Nine-Month Highlights

 

•Revenue was $7.4 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with $20.8 million in the prior-year period.
○RWA infrastructure and platform services revenue was $6.4 million in the third quarter and $9.1 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, versus zero in both corresponding prior-year periods. Digital assets and capital markets infrastructure, and advisory and onboarding contributed to the balance.
•Gross profit was $6.3 million in the quarter ended September 30, 2026 representing a gross margin of 84%, and was $13.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, representing a gross margin of 90%. This compares with 77% and 84% gross margins, respectively, in the prior-year periods. The improvement reflects a favorable revenue mix driven by the shift to high-margin RWA business.

•GAAP net loss was $10.2 million, or $0.93 per basic and diluted share, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, and $16.1 million, or $1.58 per basic and diluted share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026. Net income and earnings per share were significantly impacted by non-recurring items related to the merger, public listing, and equity compensation (detailed below).

 

  
 

 

•Adjusted EBITDA (a non-GAAP measure) was $2.3 million in the third quarter of 2026 and $(3.0) million for the first nine months of 2026. This compares to Adjusted EBITDA of $18.0 million and $8.5 million in the corresponding prior-year periods.
•Cash was $4.5 million at September 30, 2026, with positive working capital of approximately $2.6 million.
○Subsequent to quarter-end, the Company completed a $3.5 million registered direct offering.

 

“Q3 2026 was a defining quarter as we completed our transition to the public markets and accelerated our evolution into a high-margin RWA platform and major deal-origination company,” said Matthew Shaw, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of OpenWorld.

“Revenue from our RWA business grew from zero a year ago to more than $6 million in the 2026 third quarter and $9 million for the first nine months of the year, while we maintained exceptional gross margins of more than 80%. At the same time, we continue to advance a pipeline of large-scale tokenized financing programs that position OpenWorld at the center of institutional RWA activity in AI infrastructure, energy, and intellectual property.

 

“We see growing interest in RWA tokenization from some of the largest companies in the world and we expect to make consistent progress on advancing our discussions into definitive agreements. We are off to a strong start with our third quarter revenue growth, strong gross margins, and positive Adjusted EBITDA representing just the starting point for what we believe our business model can deliver.”

 

Business Evolution, Strategic Pipeline and Recent Developments

 

·During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Company completed the reverse takeover with the predecessor company and began trading on Nasdaq under the ticker “OPNW” on October 1, 2026. The Company is organized around three complementary verticals, Digital Assets & Capital Markets, Advisory & Onboarding (OpenWorld Labs), and OpenWorld Enterprise, with the latter focused on designing, structuring, deploying, and administering enterprise-grade blockchain initiatives across RWAs, sovereign infrastructure, and financial services.

 

·In October 2026, the Company entered into non-binding LOIs for more than $1.1 billion in proposed tokenized financing programs. These non-binding LOIs illustrate OpenWorld’s model of converting real-world assets - whether physical infrastructure or specialized intellectual property - into financeable, on-chain instruments at institutional scale. The company is working on definitive agreements for these LOIs and DeFi capital formation is now underway.

 

  
 

 

·The LOIs announced to date are:

 

•An up to $1 billion program with Meridian to fund the purchase and deployment of a distributed AI inference computing network powered by on-site solar energy and residential Tesla Powerwall battery storage. Meridian holds purchase rights to Tesla Powerwall systems. The initial deployment is planned for 10,000 homes, with testing targeted to begin in Q4 2026 and the first commercial site in Q1 2027. OpenWorld will serve as advisor, structuring agent, and services provider, with the financing administered on the OpenWorld Enterprise platform.

 

•An up to $130 million intellectual-property-backed transaction with BeyondAI involving the tokenization of highly specialized AI IP (eligible patents and software rights). Tokens will provide access to payments backed by the IP, while BeyondAI retains control of its technology.

 

·On November 10, 2026, tokenized OPNW shares went live on Solana via Sunrise (Wormhole Labs) and Backpack Securities. The tokenized equity complements the Company’s Nasdaq listing, supports 24/7 trading and self-custody for eligible investors, and does not involve the issuance of new shares or a capital raise by the Company.

 

·In the fourth quarter of 2026, the Company closed the acquisition of Blocksight Holdings Co. (an AI-agent platform intended to support deal structuring, asset verification, investor onboarding, and reporting across OpenWorld Enterprise programs), and completed a $3.5 million overnight registered direct offering. These actions further strengthen the Company’s platform capabilities and balance sheet as it executes on its RWA pipeline.

 

2026 Third Quarter Non-Recurring Cash and Non-Cash Expenses

 

The reported GAAP net losses for the third quarter and first nine months of 2026 were driven primarily by non-recurring items associated with the reverse takeover, public-company transition, and equity awards granted in connection with the Company’s growth and listing:

•Non-recurring cash expenses: Approximately $6.0 million of professional fees for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, related to the merger and Nasdaq listing. These costs are not expected to recur at comparable levels in future periods.
•Non-cash expenses: Stock-based compensation of $6.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, and $7.0 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, primarily related to options and restricted shares granted in late 2025 and the third quarter of 2026. There was no stock-based compensation in the prior-year periods.

 

Excluding these non-recurring items, the underlying operating performance reflected the contribution of the high-margin RWA business that scaled from zero earlier in the year.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

This press release includes Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP financial measure. The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, further adjusted to exclude certain nonrecurring stock-based compensation expenses and merger-related transaction fees. Management believes Adjusted EBITDA provides useful supplemental information about the Company’s operating performance. Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for measures prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP. A reconciliation of preliminary GAAP net loss to Adjusted EBITDA is included in this press release.
