Presidio Production Company Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

Declares increased 3Q 2026 dividend of $0.375 per share ($1.50 per share per year)

 

FORT WORTH, Texas, November 12, 2026: Presidio Production Company (NYSE: FTW) (“Presidio” or the “Company”) today announced recent highlights and results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Recent Highlights

 

●Declared increased 3Q 2026 dividend of $0.375 per share ($1.50 per share per year)
   
●Sold $17.0 million of non-producing leasehold in Oklahoma year-to-date
   
●26.1 MBoe/d third quarter net production, an increase of 15% from the second quarter, 13% oil, 58% natural gas, and 29% NGLs
   
●$29.1 million of Adjusted EBITDA and reported a net loss attributable to Presidio Production Company of $46.4 million, (reflecting a $66.5 million non-cash, unrealized loss on commodity derivatives)
   
●Established dedicated AI product engineering team under Chief Technology Officer Jason Hudak and launched The Gauntlet, the Company’s agentic software development platform
   
●Asset Intelligence programs added 3.2% to production in the third quarter

 

Management Commentary

 

“Our third quarter results reflect continued execution of our acquisition and optimization strategy,” said Will Ulrich, Chairman and Co-CEO. “Canyon Creek contributed its first full quarter, and our continued focus on improving the performance of our existing assets has allowed us to increase our annualized dividend to $1.50 per share. Each of these steps reinforces the acquisition model we are building to consolidate producing oil and gas assets.”

 

Chris Hammack, Co-CEO and Director, added: “When we closed Canyon Creek, we said the Arkoma Basin was a place to build scale through consolidation. In our first full quarter operating the assets, we moved the wells onto our production systems, right-sized compression and changed chemical vendors, and Canyon Creek helped lower our lease operating expense per Boe. In October, we added working interest in Arkoma properties where we already operate, our first step in building around that position. Land-and-expand remains the strategy.”

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operating Results

 

All financial metrics in this release reflect the successor period for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and include the Canyon Creek acquisition, which closed in July 2026. Comparisons are to the three months ended June 30, 2026 (Successor), which did not include Canyon Creek.

 

Third-quarter production averaged approximately 26.1 MBoe/d, or 2,404 MBoe for the quarter, up 15% from 22.8 MBoe/d in the second quarter, comprising approximately 13% oil, 58% natural gas and 29% NGLs. Canyon Creek drove most of the increase.

 

Total revenue was $57.3 million, up 6% from $54.0 million in the second quarter. The Company’s average realized price was $23.72 per Boe excluding derivatives and $25.49 per Boe including derivatives, reflecting a realized derivative gain of $1.77 per Boe.

 

Lease operating expense was $9.10 per Boe, down from $9.39 per Boe in the second quarter, primarily reflecting the addition of Canyon Creek production. Production taxes were $1.46 per Boe and ad valorem taxes were $0.53 per Boe, resulting in total operating expense of $11.09 per Boe.

 

Adjusted EBITDA was $29.1 million, in line with the outlook provided in August.

 

The Company reported a net loss of $50.7 million, and a net loss attributable to Presidio Production Company of $46.4 million. The net loss primarily reflects a $66.5 million non-cash, unrealized loss from marking the Company’s commodity derivatives to fair value at quarter-end, which did not represent a cash outflow during the quarter and is excluded from Adjusted EBITDA. The net loss was partially offset by a $13.8 million income tax benefit.

Capital expenditures remained minimal at approximately $0.4 million during the quarter, consistent with the Company’s low-reinvestment model.

 

Quarterly Dividend

 

The Board approved a quarterly cash dividend of $0.375 per share ($1.50 per share per year), an 11% increase from the second quarter.

 

The Q3 2026 cash dividend will be payable on December 16, 2026 to stockholders of record as of December 2, 2026.

 

Future dividends, including the amount and timing thereof, will be declared at the discretion of the Board of Directors and will depend on the Company’s financial condition, results of operations, capital requirements, and other factors the Board deems relevant.

 

Acquisitions and Growth

 

The acquisition market remains active. The Company remains focused on opportunities that meet its strategic and return criteria. Presidio’s $1.0 billion ABS Warehouse Facility, led by Goldman Sachs with Citizens Bank, N.A. participating, funded Canyon Creek with an initial $55.0 million draw.

 

Presidio continues to sell undeveloped acreage. During the third quarter, the Company sold leasehold and mineral interests in Oklahoma for $2.1 million, bringing year-to-date proceeds to $9.9 million. Since quarter-end, the Company has sold an additional $7.1 million of leasehold and mineral interests in Oklahoma, bringing year-to-date proceeds to $17.0 million.

 

The third quarter was the first full quarter of production from the Canyon Creek assets in the Arkoma Basin, acquired in July 2026 from companies controlled by Vortus Investments and additional sellers.

 

After quarter-end, the Company acquired additional working interest in Arkoma Basin properties it already operates for approximately $4.3 million, funded with cash on hand. The acquisition builds on the position established through Canyon Creek, consistent with the Company’s land-and-expand strategy.

 

AI and Asset Intelligence

 

Presidio uses AI to get more production out of the wells it already owns and to help its employees work more efficiently. The Company plans to run all of its approximately 2,300 operated wells on the same technology platform and to add future acquisitions to this platform.

 

The Asset Intelligence Group is targeting three to five percent production growth in 2026 across Presidio’s existing asset base. Its programs added approximately 3.2% to production from the Company’s assets in the third quarter. These programs include:

 

●Mainspring, the Company’s AI-powered oil and gas operating system, which monitors well-level data, flags production that falls outside expected patterns and sends recommendations to the responsible field engineer
   
●AI-controlled plunger lift on 25 wells, which adjusts each well’s plunger cycles based on its own production data rather than a fixed timer
   
●A weekend coverage program that uses AI to tell smaller weekend crews which wells to visit first

 

The Company also used AI to find shut-in wells that would be profitable to restart at lower water disposal rates and then negotiated those rates. The first of these wells came back online in October, and the Company expects to bring back ten more by year-end. In October, Presidio also began using AI to review each day’s workover jobs and flag the ones that need attention.

 

During the third quarter, Presidio built out its product engineering team under Chief Technology Officer Jason Hudak, adding four software engineers, a head of product, a product manager and an IT lead. The team built The Gauntlet, the Company’s agentic software development platform, where AI agents do much of the coding and testing so a small team can deliver software far faster than it otherwise could.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures and Reconciliations

 

This press release includes Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Unhedged EBITDA, Adjusted General and Administrative Expense, Leverage and Net Debt, which are financial measures not calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”).

 

Presidio defines Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) before (1) interest expense, net, (2) depreciation, depletion, amortization and accretion, (3) unrealized loss (gain) on derivative instruments, (4) non-cash share-based compensation, (5) non-recurring compensation expense related to our Class B Units, (6) (gain) loss on sale of assets, net, (7) loss on ARO liabilities, (8) change in fair value of earnout liability, (9) loss on early extinguishment of debt, (10) income tax expense (benefit), (11) acquisition and transaction costs, and (12) certain non-recurring costs that management does not consider indicative of ongoing performance.

 

Adjusted EBITDA is used as a supplemental financial performance measure by Presidio management and by external users of our financial statements, such as industry analysts, investors, lenders, rating agencies and others, to evaluate our operating performance and Presidio’s results of operations from period to period and against our peers without regard to financing methods, capital structure or historical cost basis. We exclude the items listed above from net income (loss) in arriving at Adjusted EBITDA because these items and related amounts can vary substantially from company to company within our industry depending upon accounting methods and book values of assets, capital structures and the method by which the assets were acquired. Adjusted EBITDA is not a measurement of our financial performance under GAAP and should not be considered as an alternative to, or more meaningful than, net income (loss) as determined in accordance with GAAP or as an indicator of our operating performance. Certain items excluded from Adjusted EBITDA are significant components in understanding and assessing a company’s financial performance, such as a company’s cost of capital and tax burden, as well as the historic costs of depreciable assets, none of which are reflected in Adjusted EBITDA. Our presentation of Adjusted EBITDA should not be construed as an inference that our results will be unaffected by unusual items. Our computations of Adjusted EBITDA may not be identical to other similarly titled measures of other companies.

 

Presidio defines Adjusted Unhedged EBITDA as Adjusted EBITDA further adjusted to remove realized gains and losses on derivative instruments. This measure is intended to show our operating results without the impact of our hedging program. Management believes Adjusted Unhedged EBITDA is an important metric that provides valuable insight into the Company’s underlying operational performance by removing the effects of financing decisions, non-cash charges, and hedging activities. Adjusted Unhedged EBITDA is a supplemental non-GAAP measure and may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies.

 

Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted Unhedged EBITDA are not substitutes for, and should be considered in addition to, net income (loss), cash flows from operating activities, or any other measure of financial performance or liquidity presented in accordance with GAAP. Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted Unhedged EBITDA as presented may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies. Cautionary Note Regarding Hydrocarbon Disclosures
