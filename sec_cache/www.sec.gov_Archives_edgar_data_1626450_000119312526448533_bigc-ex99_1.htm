Commerce Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Total ARR of $357.6 Million, an Increase of 0.5% Versus Prior Year. GMV of $9.0 Billion, an Increase of 14% Versus Prior Year. Operating Cash Flow of $9.4 Million.
 

AUSTIN, Texas – November 5, 2026 – Commerce.com, Inc. (Nasdaq: CMRC), the open commerce ecosystem built to help merchants win as AI reshapes how customers discover and buy, today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“Our third quarter results reflect another period of disciplined execution, with total ARR of $357.6 million, 14% GMV growth to $9.0 billion, and $9.4 million in operating cash flow,” said Travis Hess, CEO of Commerce. “Just as important, the strategic operating plan we initiated in September is driving greater efficiency and profitability while protecting investments in our highest-impact growth areas: product intelligence, AI-driven agentic commerce and the open architecture merchants need as commerce becomes increasingly distributed. We believe this disciplined approach positions Commerce to deliver stronger merchant outcomes and create long-term value for our customers and shareholders.”

Commerce also announced that Daniel Lentz, Chief Financial Officer and Chief Operating Officer, will depart the Company effective November 13, 2026 to pursue another opportunity. Tyler Duncan, currently Senior Vice President of Finance and Investor Relations, will serve as Interim Chief Financial Officer upon Mr. Lentz's departure. Duncan has led Commerce's financial planning and analysis, financial operations, capital markets, and investor relations functions over the past three years. Other members of the leadership team will assume Mr. Lentz's Chief Operating Officer responsibilities while the Board evaluates candidates for the permanent Chief Financial Officer and Chief Operating Officer roles.

"On behalf of the Board and our leadership team, I want to thank Daniel for his partnership, financial and operational stewardship, and contributions throughout his eight years at Commerce, including guiding our financial and operational strategy through our recent transformation," Hess added. "Tyler has been an integral leader in our finance organization. He has a deep understanding of our business, capital allocation priorities, and the investment community. We are confident he will provide continuity and help drive our next phase of disciplined execution and profitable growth."

 

 

Third Quarter Financial Highlights:

•
Total revenue was $84.2 million, down 2.1% compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025.
•
Total annual revenue run-rate (“ARR”) as of September 30, 2026 was $357.6 million, up 0.5% compared to September 30, 2025.
•
Subscription solutions revenue was $64.7 million, up 0.1% compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025.
•
Gross Merchandise Volume (GMV) was $9.0 billion, up 14% compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025.
•
Net Revenue Retention (NRR) was 94.7%, compared to 95.8% in the three months ended June 30, 2026 and 94.5% in the three months ended September 30, 2025.
•
GAAP gross margin was 74%, compared to 78% in the three months ended September 30, 2025.
•
Non-GAAP gross margin was 75%, compared to 79% in the three months ended September 30, 2025.

A reconciliation of our historical Non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP measures has been provided in the financial statement tables included in this press release, and investors are encouraged to review these reconciliations.

Annual Revenue Run-Rate

We calculate annual revenue run-rate (“ARR”) at the end of each month as the sum of: (1) contractual monthly recurring revenue at the end of the period, which includes platform subscription fees, invoiced growth adjustments, feed management subscription fees, recurring professional services revenue, and other recurring revenue, multiplied by twelve to prospectively annualize recurring revenue, and (2) the sum of the trailing twelve-month non-recurring and variable revenue, which includes one-time partner integrations, one-time fees, payments revenue share, and any other revenue that is non-recurring and variable.

 

Gross Merchandise Volume (GMV)

 

Exhibit 99.1

Gross Merchandise Volume (“GMV”) represents the total dollar value of completed checkout transactions facilitated through the Commerce platform during the reporting period, including shipping and taxes. GMV is reported on a gross basis before deducting refunds or discounts. GMV is not a measure of revenue.

 

Net Revenue Retention (NRR)

Net Revenue Retention (“NRR”) measures our ability to retain and expand revenue from existing customers over time. NRR is calculated by dividing total billings and allocated partner revenue from a cohort of customers during the trailing twelve-month period by the total billings and allocated partner revenue from the same customer cohort in the corresponding prior-year period. NRR reflects the impact of customer expansion and contraction and excludes revenue from customers added after the prior twelve-month period.

Adjusted EBITDA

We define Adjusted EBITDA as our GAAP net income (loss), excluding the impact of stock-based compensation expense and related payroll tax costs, amortization of intangible assets, acquisition-related costs, restructuring charges, depreciation, gain on convertible note extinguishment, interest income, interest expense, other expense, and our provision for income taxes. Acquisition-related costs include contingent compensation arrangements entered into in connection with acquisitions and achieved earnout related to an acquisition.
 

Restructuring charges include severance benefits, right-of-use asset impairments, lease termination gain, contract costs, accelerated depreciation, professional services costs, and other related costs.

 

Depreciation includes depreciation expenses related to the Company's fixed assets.

 

The most directly comparable GAAP measure is net income (loss).

Non-GAAP Operating Income

We define Non-GAAP operating income as our GAAP income (loss) from operations, excluding the impact of stock-based compensation expense and related payroll tax costs, amortization of intangible assets, acquisition-related costs, and restructuring charges. The most directly comparable GAAP measure is our income (loss) from operations.

 

Non-GAAP Net Income

We define Non-GAAP net income as our GAAP net income (loss), excluding the impact of stock-based compensation expense and related payroll tax costs, amortization of intangible assets, acquisition-related costs, restructuring charges, and gain on convertible note extinguishment. The most directly comparable GAAP measure is our net income (loss).

 

Non-GAAP Basic and Diluted Net Income per Share

We define Non-GAAP basic and diluted net income (loss) per share as our Non-GAAP net income, defined above, divided by our basic and diluted GAAP weighted average shares outstanding, respectively. The most directly comparable GAAP measures are our basic, and diluted net income (loss) per share.

 

Free Cash Flow

 

Exhibit 99.1

We define free cash flow as net cash provided by operating activities, less purchases of capitalized internal-use software, leasehold improvements, and property and equipment, collectively referred to as capital expenditures, and cash paid for website domain name. The most directly comparable GAAP measure is net cash provided by operating activities.
