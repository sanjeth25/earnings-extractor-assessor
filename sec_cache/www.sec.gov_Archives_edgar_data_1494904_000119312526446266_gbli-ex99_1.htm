For Immediate Release — November 4, 2026

Global Indemnity Group, LLC Reports Financial Results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026

 

 

 

Wilmington, Del., (November 4, 2026) — Global Indemnity Group, LLC (Nasdaq: GBLI) (the "Company") today reported financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

For the three months ended September 30, 2026, net income was $8.9 million, or $0.61 per share, compared to $12.5 million, or $0.86 per share, in 2025. Current accident year underwriting income was $5.6 million, with a loss ratio of 53.0% and a current accident year combined ratio of 94.8%. Belmont Core gross written premiums of $102.6 million were 5.5% lower than 2025. Pretax Adjusted Operating Contribution was $20.1 million and Adjusted Return on Equity was 12.8%.

For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, net income was $24.2 million, or $1.65 per share, compared to $18.9 million, or $1.30 per share, in 2025. Current accident year underwriting income increased to $16.9 million from $5.4 million in 2025, with a loss ratio of 53.8% and a current accident year combined ratio of 94.7%. Belmont Core gross written premiums of $316.4 million were in line with 2025. Pretax Adjusted Operating Contribution was $60.0 million and Adjusted Return on Equity was 12.6%.

Jessica E. Buss, CEO of Global Indemnity, commented, “I joined GBLI as CEO this week. I studied this company closely before accepting the role, and I came in knowing exactly where we stand and what we can become. Our results for the first nine months ran well ahead of last year. The third quarter came in softer, as we held to our underwriting standards while competitors cut property rates, and we owe our shareholders a plain account of that. We also owe them a plan. I am confident that, under my leadership, Katalyx will expand its distribution substantially over the next five years; in my first 48 hours, I set that expansion in motion for 2027. We will put Kaleidoscope to work for our wholesalers, agents and underwriters, and Belmont will materially improve its operating efficiency. Yes, the specialty market is softening, but GBLI meets it with 23 years of top-quartile loss ratios, industry-leading capital reserves, no debt, extremely modest operating leverage and long-standing agency relationships. Many leading E&S wholesalers are now eager to work with Katalyx and its Kaleidoscope technology. Going forward, we will hold ourselves to one measure: sustained growth in premiums, earnings and book value per share at rates far beyond anything GBLI achieved since its founding.”

Highlights of Consolidated Results

 

Operating Performance
•
Operating income of $26.4 million, or $1.81 per share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $21.8 million, or $1.51 per share, in 2025.
•
Current accident year underwriting income was $5.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $10.2 million in 2025. A $1.3 million decline in net earned premiums and a $3.0 million effect of the higher loss ratio drove the decrease. The loss ratio rose to 53.0% from 50.1% in 2025, a quarter with unusually few severe property losses. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, current accident year underwriting income rose to $16.9 million from $5.4 million in 2025 which included $15.8 million of losses from California Wildfires.
•
Net investment income of $43.1 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $47.4 million in 2025, resulting from lower limited partnership returns and lower average yields on the fixed income portfolio due to lower U.S. Treasury rates. In September 2026, the Company began lengthening duration by redeploying U.S. Treasuries into higher-yielding securities, lifting the fixed income portfolio book yield to 4.8% and duration of 1.5 years at September 30, 2026, from 4.3% and 1.0 years at December 31, 2025. Average credit quality remains AA-.

 

 

 

Belmont Core Gross Written Premiums
•
Belmont Core gross written premiums of $102.6 million for the third quarter were down 5.5% compared to $108.5 million in 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, Belmont Core gross written premiums of $316.4 million were flat compared to $316.7 million in 2025. Excluding terminated business, Belmont Core gross written premiums grew 1.5% for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.
o
Wholesale Commercial: $62.1 million for the third quarter, down 8.6% compared to 2025 and $193.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, down 4% compared to the same period last year. The Company maintained its pricing and return standards amid competitive market conditions, especially in the property market. Overall rates decreased 3% for the 3rd quarter and 1% for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.
o
Assumed E&S Reinsurance: $15.5 million for the third quarter, flat compared to 2025 and $48.2 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, up 25% compared to the same period last year driven by new treaties incepting during 2025 and 2026.
o
Vacant Express: $11.5 million for the third quarter, up 1% compared to 2025 and $36.0 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, up 4% compared to the same period last year driven by overall rate increase, market opportunities in targeted classes and new agency appointments.
o
Collectibles: $5.8 million for the third quarter, up 14% compared to 2025 and $15.2 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, up 13% compared to the same period last year driven by market opportunities in targeted classes and organic growth.
o
Specialty Products: $7.7 million for the third quarter, down 9.5% compared to 2025 and $23.3 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, down 17.6% compared to the same period last year, reflecting the run-off of terminated products and competitive market conditions. Excluding terminated business, premiums increased 4.5% in the third quarter and 0.5% for the nine months.

 

Capital Position and Book Value

•
Common shareholders' equity of $700.9 million at September 30, 2026 compared to $702.6 million at December 31, 2025, reflecting unrealized losses in the fixed income portfolio of $12.7 million, net of tax.
•
Book value per share of $47.87 declined 2.2% from $48.96 at December 31, 2025, after $1.05 per share of common dividends and a $0.89 per share unrealized decline in the fixed income portfolio. Book value per share plus cumulative dividends and excluding AOCI rose 1.4% to $58.86.
•
Dividends of $15.4 million were paid during the nine months ended September 30, 2026. Since its 2003 initial public offering, the Company has returned $664.9 million to shareholders, including $522.2 million in share repurchases and $142.7 million in dividends and distributions.
•
AM Best affirmed Global Indemnity Group's "A" (Excellent) rating for its U.S. insurance subsidiaries in August 2026.

About Global Indemnity Group, LLC

Global Indemnity Group, LLC (Nasdaq: GBLI) is a publicly traded holding company with a diversified portfolio of property and casualty insurance-related operating companies, organized in three groups:

Katalyx Holdings LLC

•
Four agencies that source, underwrite and service primary and assumed reinsurance business: Penn-America Insurance Services, LLC; Valyn Re LLC; J.H. Ferguson & Associates, LLC (including Vacant Express); and Collectibles Insurance Services, LLC.
•
Two specialized insurance service businesses: Sayata, an AI-enabled digital marketplace and agency for small commercial insurance; and Liberty Insurance Adjustment Agency, Inc., which evaluates and adjusts claims and provides related services.

 

Belmont Holdings GX, Inc.

Five statutory insurance carriers, each rated “A” (Excellent) by AM Best: Penn-America Insurance Company, United National Insurance Company, Penn-Patriot Insurance Company, Diamond State Insurance Company and Penn-Star Insurance Company.

 

Kaleidoscope Insurance Technologies, Inc.

Kaleidoscope builds GBLI’s proprietary AI-enabled underwriting, policy and data platform, which serves Katalyx’s agencies, GBLI’s agency partners, the Belmont carriers and the Company’s broader digital initiatives.

 

For more information, visit www.gbli.com.
