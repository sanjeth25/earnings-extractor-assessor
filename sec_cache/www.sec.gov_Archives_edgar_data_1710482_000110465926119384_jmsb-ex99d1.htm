3rd Quarter Net Income Increases 39.5% Versus Year Ago Quarter

Funding, Asset Quality, and Capital Position Company for Anticipated Growth

​

Reston, VA – John Marshall Bancorp, Inc. (Nasdaq: JMSB) (the “Company”), parent company of John Marshall Bank (the “Bank”), reported net income of $7.5 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 compared to $5.4 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, an increase of $2.1 million or 39.5%.  Diluted earnings per common share were $0.53 for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 compared to $0.38 for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, also an increase of 39.5%. Annualized return on average assets (“ROAA”) was 1.23% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 compared to 0.94% for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. Annualized return on average equity (“ROAE”) was 10.78% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 compared to 8.31% for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.

​

Selected Highlights

●Pending Merger – On September 8, 2026, the Company and Eagle Financial Services, Inc. (“Eagle”), parent company of Bank of Clarke, jointly announced the signing of a definitive merger agreement. Under the terms of the agreement, at closing, Eagle will merge with and into the Company in an all-stock transaction. The combined entity is projected to have approximately $4.3 billion in assets and 23 banking offices. The transaction is expected to close during the first quarter of 2027, subject to satisfaction of customary closing conditions, including regulatory and shareholder approvals.
●Earnings Growth Momentum – Net income of $7.5 million and earnings per diluted common share of $0.53 for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 represented a 7.4% increase over the $7.0 million net income and $0.50 earnings per diluted common share reported for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. The quarter ended September 30, 2026 represented the ninth consecutive quarter of net income growth and marked the highest level of net income since the fourth quarter of 2022. During the most recent quarter, the Company incurred $332 thousand of merger and acquisition expenses associated with the pending acquisition of Eagle, while the preceding quarter included an $835 thousand gain recognized on a sale of the Company’s equity interest in one of its equity investment units. Adjusting for these non-recurring items, core net income (Non-GAAP) was $7.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, representing a 22.7% quarterly increase over the $6.4 million recorded in the linked quarter. Core earnings per diluted common share (Non-GAAP) also increased over the same period from $0.45 to $0.55, representing a 22.8% quarterly increase. Refer to “Explanation of Non-GAAP Financial Measures” and the “Reconciliation of Certain Non-GAAP Financial Measures” table for further details about financial measures used in this release that were determined by methods other than in accordance with GAAP.
●Significant Increase in Net Interest Income – For the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company reported net interest income of $17.8 million, representing a $0.5 million or 10.4% annualized increase over the linked quarter and a $2.2 million or 14.0% increase over the prior-year quarter.
●Strong Core Deposit Growth – The Company remains focused on driving value through core deposit growth. Total deposits increased $71.3 million or 14.2% annualized during the most recent quarter. Core deposits increased $83.3 million or 19.8% annualized. Wholesale funding sources decreased $52.0 million or 12.5% during the third quarter.  For the twelve months ended September 30, 2026, total deposits increased $95.4 million or 4.8%.
●Net Interest Margin – Net interest margin grew 19 basis points from 2.72% for the third quarter of 2025 to 2.91% for the third quarter of 2026. Net interest margin declined by eight basis points during the most recent quarter, mainly due to a seven basis point increase in the cost of interest-bearing deposits.
●Loan Growth – Loans, net of unearned income, increased $73.0 million or 3.8% from September 30, 2025 to September 30, 2026. The Company’s loan portfolio, net of unearned income, at September 30, 2026

●Positive Operating Leverage – Total revenue (net interest income plus non-interest income) grew 11.1% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 relative to the quarter ended September 30, 2025, while non-interest expense decreased 3.9% over the same period. ●Strong Asset Quality – Overall credit quality of the loan portfolio remains exceptional. ●Growing Book Value per Share and Dividends – Book value per share increased from $18.27 as of September 30, 2025 to $19.64 as of September 30, 2026, a 7.5% increase.  On October 27, 2026, the Company’s Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.10 per share on the Company’s common stock.  The dividend is payable on December 2, 2026 to shareholders of record at the close of business on November 10, 2026.
●Robust Capitalization – Each of the Bank’s regulatory capital ratios remained well in excess of the regulatory well-capitalized thresholds as of September 30, 2026.

Balance Sheet, Liquidity and Credit Quality

The Company carried balance sheet growth momentum into the third quarter of 2026. Total assets were $2.44 billion at September 30, 2026, $2.40 billion at June 30, 2026, and $2.32 billion at September 30, 2025.  Total assets increased $34.5 million or 5.7% annualized from June 30, 2026 and $112.3 million or 4.8% from September 30, 2025.

Total loans, net of unearned income, stayed relatively unchanged at September 30, 2026 at $2.01 billion compared to June 30, 2026 and increased $73.0 million or 3.8% from $1.94 billion at September 30, 2025.  The increase in loans over the preceding twelve months was primarily attributable to the growth in residential mortgage loans, investor real estate loans and construction & development loans.  Refer to the Loan, Deposit and Borrowing Detail table for further information.

​

The carrying value of the Company’s fixed income securities portfolio was $219.2 million at September 30, 2026, $213.7 million at June 30, 2026, and $205.7 million at September 30, 2025.  During the most recent quarter, the Company purchased eight fixed income securities, designated as available-for-sale, with a total carrying amount of $18.5 million and a weighted average purchase yield of 4.68%. Fixed income securities which matured during the most recent quarter had an average yield of 2.58%. As of September 30, 2026, 96.0% of our bond portfolio carried the implied guarantee of the United States government or one of its agencies.  At September 30, 2026, 75.2% of the fixed income portfolio was invested in amortizing bonds, which provide the Company with a source of steady cash flow.  At September 30, 2026, the fixed income portfolio had an estimated weighted average life of 3.9 years.  The

The Company did not have an allowance for credit losses on held-to-maturity securities as of September 30, 2026 or December 31, 2025.  As of September 30, 2026, 93.0% of our held-to-maturity portfolio carried the implied guarantee of the United States government or one of its agencies.

The Company’s balance sheet remains highly liquid.  The Company’s liquidity position, defined as the sum of cash, unencumbered securities and available secured borrowing capacity, totaled $809.9 million as of September 30, 2026 compared to $827.2 million as of June 30, 2026 and represented 33.2% and 34.4% of total assets, respectively. In addition to available secured borrowing capacity, the Bank had available federal funds lines of $110.0 million at September 30, 2026.

Total deposits increased $71.3 million or 14.2% annualized to $2.06 billion at September 30, 2026 compared to $1.99 billion at June 30, 2026, and increased $95.4 million or 4.8% from $1.97 billion at September 30, 2025.  During the most recent quarter, total interest-bearing deposits increased $51.2 million or 13.2% annualized, while total non-interest bearing deposits increased $20.1 million or 17.7% annualized. Detail on the deposit activity can be seen in the Loan, Deposit and Borrowing Detail table.  As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $760.5 million of deposits that were not insured or not collateralized compared to $691.5 million and $682.8 million at December 31, 2025 and September 30, 2025, respectively.

Federal Home Loan Bank (“FHLB”) advances remained unchanged at $56.0 million as of September 30, 2026 compared to June 30, 2026 and September 30, 2025.  As of September 30, 2026, the FHLB advances had a weighted average fixed interest rate of 3.89%.  In addition to outstanding FHLB advances, total borrowings as of September 30, 2026 included subordinated debt totaling $24.9 million. During the most recent quarter, the Company fully paid off $40.0 million of federal funds purchased, which were outstanding as of June 30, 2026.

Shareholders’ equity increased $17.4 million or 6.7% to $277.1 million at September 30, 2026 compared to $259.7 million at September 30, 2025. Book value per share was $19.64 as of September 30, 2026 compared to $18.27 as of September 30, 2025, an increase of 7.5%. The year-over-year increase in shareholders’ equity and book value per share was primarily due to the Company’s earnings over the previous twelve months, partially offset by an increase in accumulated other comprehensive loss, resulting from a decrease in the market value of our available-for-sale investment portfolio, along with cash dividends paid and a reduction of additional paid-in capital due to the Company’s share repurchases during the period.

The Bank’s capital ratios remained well above regulatory thresholds for well-capitalized banks. As of September 30, 2026, the Bank’s total risk-based capital ratio was 17.1%, compared to 16.3% and 16.6% at December 31, 2025 and September 30, 2025, respectively.

During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Company designated one commercial business SBA 7(a) loan as non-accrual. As of September 30, 2026, the total outstanding principal amount of the loan was $267 thousand and was fully guaranteed by the SBA. The Company charged off the unguaranteed portion of the loan, in the total amount of $89 thousand, during the second quarter of 2026 and submitted a reimbursement claim to the SBA for the guaranteed portion. We expect the SBA to pay its guarantee and resolve our one non-accruing loan. During the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company recorded no charge-offs and had no other real estate owned assets as of September 30, 2026.

At September 30, 2026, the allowance for loan credit losses was $19.8 million or 0.98% of outstanding loans, net of unearned income, compared to $20.2 million or 1.00% of outstanding loans, net of unearned income, at June 30, 2026. The decrease in the allowance for credit losses during the most recent quarter was predominantly driven by a change in the loan portfolio mix coupled with the improvement in forecasted economic variables used in the quantitative portion of the allowance. Management believes the current allowance for credit losses is appropriate given the composition and performance of the loan portfolio.

At September 30, 2026, the allowance for credit losses on unfunded loan commitments was $1.2 million as compared to $1.1 million at June 30, 2026.

Income Statement Review

Quarterly Results

The Company reported net income of $7.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $2.1 million or 39.5% when compared to $5.4 million for the third quarter of 2025.  During the most recent quarter, the Company incurred $332 thousand of merger and acquisition expenses associated with the pending Eagle acquisition. Adjusting for these expenses, core net income (Non-GAAP) was $7.8 million, representing a 44.4% year-over-year increase.

For the three months ended September 30, 2026, net interest income increased $2.2 million or 14.0% to $17.8 million compared to $15.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. During the same period, interest income grew $2.4 million or 8.1%, driven by higher interest income on loans and securities, while interest expense increased $0.2 million or 1.2%, predominantly due to higher interest expense on core time deposits.

The annualized net interest margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 2.91% compared to 2.72% for the same period in 2025. The increase in net interest margin was primarily due to increases in average balances and yields of the loan portfolio coupled with lower rates on interest-bearing deposits.

4

The yield on interest-earning assets was 5.13% for the third quarter of 2026 compared to 5.06% for the same period in 2025 primarily due to a ten basis point increase in loan yield coupled with a 50 basis point increase in securities yield.  These increases were partially offset by a 69 basis point decrease in yield on interest-bearing deposits in other banks, as a result of a decline in the federal funds rate during the preceding twelve months.  Average loans increased by $99.7 million between the three months ended September 30, 2026 and the three months ended September 30, 2025, which was primarily attributable to origination volume in the residential mortgage loans, investor real estate loans and construction & development loans subsequent to September 30, 2025. The cost of interest-bearing liabilities was 3.20% for the third quarter of 2026 compared to 3.37% for the same quarter in the prior year driven by the 18 basis point decline in rates on interest-bearing deposits. Rates declined across all deposit categories, most notably in time deposits, money market accounts and NOW accounts, which declined by 29 basis points, 20 basis points, and nine basis points, respectively.  

The Company recorded a $318 thousand recovery of provision for credit losses for the third quarter of 2026 compared to a provision expense of $356 thousand for the third quarter of 2025.  Provision for credit losses on funded loans was a recovery of $397 thousand, while provision for credit losses on unfunded loan commitments was an expense of $79 thousand during the three months ended September 30, 2026.  The recovery of provision for credit losses on funded loans during the most recent quarter reflected a change in the loan portfolio mix in combination with the improved forecasts of the unemployment rate and gross domestic product as compared to June 30, 2026.

Non-interest income decreased $385 thousand or 59.0% during the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025. The main drivers of this decrease were a $141 thousand decrease in mark-to-market adjustments on investments related to the Company’s non-qualified deferred compensation plan, a $101 thousand decrease in swap fee income, an $82 thousand decrease in the gain on sale of SBA 7(a) loans, and a $58 thousand decrease in bankers’ insurance commission due to the sale of our Bearing Insurance investment unit during the second quarter of 2026.

Non-interest expense decreased $355 thousand or 3.9% during the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025, primarily resulting from a $542 thousand decrease in salaries and employee benefits due to a lower incentive compensation accrual, a decrease in mark-to-market adjustments on the Company’s non-qualified deferred compensation plan and higher commercial loan origination costs due to higher loan origination volume since the prior year period. Incentive compensation accruals can fluctuate materially from quarter to quarter, based upon the Company’s financial performance and conditions measured against, among other evaluation criteria, our strategic plan and budget. At the end of each year, the ultimate determination of the incentive compensation is approved by the Board of Directors. These decreases were partially offset by the previously mentioned merger and acquisition expenses, incurred during the most recent quarter, related to the pending Eagle acquisition. Core non-interest expense (Non-GAAP) decreased $687 thousand or 7.6% during the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the same period in 2025.

For the three months ended September 30, 2026, annualized non-interest expense to average assets was 1.41% compared to 1.57% for the three months ended September 30, 2025.  This decrease was due to a decline in non-interest expense in combination with the growth in average assets during the period. Adjusted for merger and acquisition expenses incurred during the most recent quarter, annualized core non-interest expense to average assets (Non-GAAP) was 1.36%.

​

For the three months ended September 30, 2026, the efficiency ratio declined to 48.1% compared to 55.6% for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The improvement in the efficiency ratio was due to an 11.1% growth in total revenue coupled with a 3.9% decrease in non-interest expense over the period. Adjusted for merger and acquisition expenses incurred during the most recent quarter, core efficiency ratio (Non-GAAP) was 46.2%.

​

Annualized ROAA for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was 1.23% and annualized ROAE was 10.78% compared to 0.94% and 8.31%, respectively, for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted for the merger and acquisition expenses incurred during the most recent quarter, annualized core ROAA (Non-GAAP) and annualized core ROAE (Non-GAAP) were 1.27% and 11.15%, respectively.

About John Marshall Bancorp, Inc.
