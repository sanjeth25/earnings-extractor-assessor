Parsons Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

Q3 2026 Key Takeaways

▪
Revenue, profitability, and cash flow exceeded commitments, with adjusted EBITDA and margin reaching record levels
▪
Continued strong demand across both segments, with contract awards increasing 14% year-over-year and a 1.1x consolidated book-to-bill ratio, including a 53% increase in Federal Solutions contract awards
▪
Record total and funded backlog
▪
Third-quarter financial results, combined with growing backlog, strong bookings, and a robust pipeline, underscore the resilience of the company’s portfolio and provide a solid foundation for sustained organic growth across both segments

Q3 2026 Highlights

▪
Achieved a 1.1x book-to-bill ratio, maintaining a TTM ratio of at least 1.0x in every quarter since 2019 IPO; both segments delivered a third-quarter ratio of 1.0x or greater
▪
Q3 revenue of $1.7 billion increased 2% year-over-year and decreased 1% on an organic basis, exceeding commitments
▪
Revenue growth of 5% excluding confidential contract; 2% on an organic basis
▪
Net income of $54 million decreased $10 million. Net income was impacted by transaction-related expenses
▪
Adjusted EBITDA increased 6% to a record $168 million; margin expanded 40 basis points to a record 10.2%
▪
Total and funded backlog increased 6% and 7%, respectively, both reaching record levels of $9.4 billion and $6.8 billion
▪
Signed a definitive purchase agreement and finalizing novation for the divestiture of the remote-location contracts; made measurable progress on the Critical Infrastructure joint venture program
▪
Narrowing and reiterating mid-point of fiscal year 2026 revenue and adjusted EBITDA guidance ranges; reiterating operating cash flow guidance range

 

Chantilly, VA – October 28, 2026, Parsons Corporation (NYSE: PSN) today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

CEO Commentary

“We delivered a strong third quarter, exceeding our commitments with record adjusted EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA margin, and robust operating cash flow and bookings across both Federal Solutions and Critical Infrastructure. This performance highlights the resilience of our diversified portfolio and the strength of our differentiated solutions and products. Strong awards, record total and funded backlog, and a robust pipeline provide a solid foundation for continued organic growth,” said Carey Smith, chair, president, and chief executive officer.

 

“We also continued to strengthen our portfolio, making meaningful progress on the strategic initiatives we outlined last quarter. While we continue to navigate procurement timing and geopolitical uncertainty, the alignment of our differentiated capabilities with our customers’ most critical national security and infrastructure needs reinforces our confidence in Parsons’ outlook. We remain focused on disciplined execution, converting our strong pipeline and recent awards into profitable growth, and delivering long-term value for our customers, employees, and shareholders.”

Significant Contract Wins

Parsons continues to win new business across both segments. During the third quarter of 2026, the company received three contract actions valued at more than $100 million each and continued to expand its use of Other Transaction Agreements (OTAs).

▪
Received an increase in the ceiling value of the five-year, sole-source OTA with U.S. Cyber Command to $750 million. Under this agreement, the company has begun full-rate production of the Joint Cyber Hunt Kit, or JCHK. In total, the company has booked $250 million on this agreement.
▪
Awarded a 66-month, $495 million ceiling value contract for a national security customer to acquire and deploy advanced communications, power, and software capabilities. The company booked $37 million on this contract during the third quarter.
▪
Booked $234 million of the $514 million Missile Defense Agency’s (MDA) Technical, Engineering, Advisory, and Management Support (TEAMS) – NEXT Systems Engineering contract two-year option period. For the first nine months of 2026, the company has booked $429 million on this contract extension.
▪
Awarded a five-year, $97 million Other Transaction Agreement by a government customer to deliver technology to simulate radio frequency and cyber conditions. The company booked $4 million on this agreement during the third quarter.
▪
Awarded a five-year, $95 million Other Transaction Agreement by a government customer to modernize software supporting early-warning capabilities. The company booked $19 million on this agreement during the third quarter.
▪
Awarded an OTA by the Department of War for solutions related to the Pacific Command theater of operations. This three-year OTA has a ceiling value of $95 million and expands the company’s footprint in PACOM, enabling continued deployment of exquisite solutions that advance mission success and protect national security. The company booked $30 million on this agreement during the third quarter.
▪
Awarded a 75-month, $86 million OTA by Army Contracting Command – Rock Island, in coordination with Air Force Civil Engineering Center. Under this agreement, which represents new work for the company, Parsons will serve as the Owner’s Advisor for the development of U.S. Space Command's new headquarters at Redstone Arsenal in Huntsville, Alabama, providing comprehensive program and construction management support during planning, design, construction, and operationalization. This OTA will oversee one of the Department of War’s largest progressive design-build initiatives to date and is one of the Air Force’s first uses of an Owner’s Advisor delivery approach. The company booked the full amount on this agreement during the third quarter.
▪
Awarded a $70 million single-award task order under the Air Base, Air Defense IDIQ contract. Under this task order, Parsons will support the integration, testing, deployment, sustainment, maintenance, and operational transition of point-defense systems designed to defend personnel, aircraft, installations, and other critical assets from emerging aerial threats through Europe and Africa. This contract has a four-year period of performance with three 12-month option periods. The company booked $46 million on this contract during the third quarter.

CFO Transition

Matt Ofilos, Parsons’ Chief Financial Officer, has notified the Company of his decision to resign from his position, effective November 6, 2026, to pursue another professional opportunity. Mr. Ofilos’ resignation is not the result of any disagreement with the Company on any matter relating to the Company’s operations, policies, or practices.

 

George Ball, who served as Parsons’ Chief Financial Officer from 2008 to 2022 and has served on the Company’s Board of Directors since 2022, has been appointed interim Chief Financial Officer, effective November 6, 2026.

These financial measures are calculated and presented on the basis of methodologies other than in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles ("Non-GAAP Measures"). Parsons has provided these Non-GAAP Measures to adjust for, among other things, the impact of amortization expenses related to our acquisitions, costs associated with a loss or gain on the disposal or sale of property, plant and equipment, restructuring and related expenses, costs associated with mergers and acquisitions, software implementation costs, legal and settlement costs, and other costs considered non-operational in nature. These items have been Adjusted because they are not considered core to the company’s business or otherwise not considered operational or because these charges are non-cash or non-recurring. The company presents these Non-GAAP Measures because management believes that they are meaningful to understanding Parsons’s performance during the periods presented and the company’s ongoing business. Non-GAAP Measures are not prepared in accordance with GAAP and therefore are not necessarily comparable to similarly titled metrics or the financial results of other companies. These Non-GAAP Measures should be considered a supplement to, not a substitute for, or superior to, the corresponding financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP.
