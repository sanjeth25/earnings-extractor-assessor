IBEX Reports Record Start to Fiscal 2027, Raises Full Year Guidance

•Quarterly revenue up 16% over the prior year to a record $175.2 million, marking the seventh consecutive quarter of double-digit organic growth
•Record first quarter net income, adjusted net income, adjusted EBITDA, EPS, adjusted EPS, operating cash flow, and free cash flow
•First quarter diluted EPS grew 20% versus prior year quarter to $0.99, and adjusted EPS grew 21% to $1.10
•Won four new logos in the quarter, including two in FinTech
•Strong momentum continues with our Sierra partnership, with four new AI agent wins since quarter ended with robust pipeline
•Raises full year revenue and adjusted EBITDA guidance

WASHINGTON, DC— November 5, 2026 —IBEX Limited (“ibex”) (Nasdaq: IBEX), a global leader in outsourced business services and AI-powered customer experience solutions, today announced financial results for its first fiscal quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“ibex delivered a record-breaking start to fiscal 2027, with first quarter revenue growing 16% organically to $175.2 million. This performance marked our seventh straight quarter of double-digit growth, the highest adjusted EBITDA quarter in our history, and drove adjusted EPS growth of over 21%,” said Bob Dechant, ibex CEO. “Our strong results reflect the significant differentiation we have built at ibex, enabling our new logo engine to win at an unprecedented rate while we consistently take market share away from our competition with our existing clients. We are also benefitting from our new vector of growth through our AI agent solution, powered by our strategic partnership with Sierra.”



First Quarter Financial Performance
Revenue
•Revenue of $175.2 million, an increase of 15.9% from $151.2 million in the prior year quarter, was driven by broad-based growth across four verticals: HealthTech (+39.9%), Travel, Transportation and Logistics (+16.4%), Technology (+9.8%), Retail & E-commerce (+8.7%), with additional growth in our digital acquisition business and accelerating growth in our AI Agent solutions.

Net Income and Earnings Per Share
•Net income increased to $14.8 million compared to $12.0 million in the prior year quarter. Net income was favorably impacted by revenue growth in our higher margin offshore regions, growth in our higher margin AI agent and digital acquisition business, and lower selling, general, and administrative expenses as a percentage of revenue.
•Diluted earnings per share increased to $0.99 compared to $0.82 in the prior year quarter.
•Net income margin increased to 8.4% compared to 8.0% in the prior year quarter.
•Non-GAAP adjusted net income increased to $16.4 million compared to $13.1 million in the prior year quarter (see Exhibit 1 for reconciliation).
•Non-GAAP adjusted diluted earnings per share increased to $1.10 compared to $0.90 in the prior year quarter (see Exhibit 1 for reconciliation).
Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA
•Adjusted EBITDA increased to $25.2 million compared to $19.5 million in the prior year quarter (see Exhibit 2 for reconciliation).
•Adjusted EBITDA margin increased to 14.4% compared to 12.9% in the prior year quarter (see Exhibit 2 for reconciliation).

Cash Flow and Balance Sheet
•Capital expenditures were $7.2 million compared to $7.6 million in the prior year quarter. The capital expenditures during the current quarter were driven by capacity expansion to meet strong demand in our highest margin offshore regions.
•Cash flow from operating activities increased to a first quarter record of $16.3 million compared to $15.7 million in the prior year quarter.
•Free cash flow of $9.2 million compared to $8.0 million in the prior year quarter (see Exhibit 3 for reconciliation).
•Net cash was $35.0 million compared to net cash of $30.9 million as of June 30, 2026 (see Exhibit 4 for reconciliation).
•Repurchased 122,806 shares for $4.4 million at an average price of $35.92.

First Quarter Review and Fiscal 2027 Business Outlook
“Fiscal 2027 is off to an exceptional start with first quarter performance that included records across nearly every key operating metric, including revenue, adjusted EBITDA, EPS, and free cash flow. Our financial results were driven by stellar growth from new logos and further augmented by expansion in our embedded client base. This combination makes us stronger as we grow, underscoring the long term and durable value of our differentiated strategy. Looking ahead, this momentum gives us increased confidence in our outlook for fiscal 2027 to continue to drive double-digit growth and year-over-year margin expansion. In response, we are raising both revenue and adjusted EBITDA guidance for the year,” said Taylor Greenwald, CFO of ibex.

Fiscal Year 2027 Guidance
•    Revenue is now expected to be in the range of $720 to $735 million, up 12-14% versus previous guidance of $700 to $715 million for 9-11% growth.
•Adjusted EBITDA is now expected to be in the range of $96 to $99 million, up 17-20% versus previous guidance of $90 to $94 million for 9-14% growth.
•Capital expenditures for the year are expected to be approximately $30 million, at the high end of the previous $25 to $30 million range given the increased growth expectations.

We define adjusted earnings per share as adjusted net income divided by weighted average diluted shares outstanding.

EXHIBIT 2: EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, and adjusted EBITDA margin

Adjusted EBITDA margin is a non-GAAP profitability measure that represents adjusted EBITDA divided by revenue.
