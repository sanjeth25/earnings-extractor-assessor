NEWS RELEASE

CONTANGO SILVER & GOLD

Contango Announces Results for the Quarter Ended September 30, 2026

FAIRBANKS, AK - (November 13, 2026) - Contango Silver & Gold Inc. ("Contango" or the "Company") (NYSE American / TSX: CTGO) announced today that it filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission its Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 (“Q3-2026”).

Rick Van Nieuwenhuyse, the Company’s Chief Executive Officer said, “The focus for the third quarter was to continue to advance and de-risk our portfolio of advanced-stage exploration projects. On the mining front at Manh Choh, the Peak Gold JV completed the transition from the north pit to the south pit while significant improvements were also made to the milling facility to accommodate the higher copper and total sulfur content of the high-grade sulfide ore. Operational enhancements continue to be made ahead of our fourth 2026 campaign, scheduled to commence in mid-November with anticipated production of 13,000 to 15,000 gold equivalent ounces net to Contango, keeping us on track to meet the lower end of 2026 production guidance of 40,000 gold equivalent ounces. The Company expects to provide 2027 production and cost guidance later in December.”

Mr. Van Nieuwenhuyse continued, "At Kitsault Valley we have now completed 56,000 meters of drilling, roughly 40% more meters than planned, while remaining on budget. We will continue to release assay results in batches as they are received. We expect to have all assays received and QAQC’s by January 2027 and then complete an updated mineral resource estimate (“MRE”) in the first half of 2027. The updated MRE will form the basis of an Initial Assessment (“IA”) targeted for release in late 2027. The IA will outline a mine development plan for the silver rich Dolly Varden and gold-silver rich Homestake areas located in the Kitsault Valley. At Johnson Tract, we completed construction of the 2.6-mile access road connecting the existing camp to the proposed portal site, along with the portal laydown area, and expanded the camp footprint to ready the site for expansion and year-round operations. Meanwhile we are continuing to advance permitting through the FAST-41 program – please monitor our progress on the FAST-41 dashboard at the following link: Permitting Dashboard. At Lucky Shot we completed our surface and underground drilling programs, totaling over 11,000 meters in addition to 536 meters of underground tunnel development. In mid-September we commenced the next phase of underground drilling with two rigs operating 24-7 as well as additional tunnel development for exploration. We expect to complete this work by February 2027. Once all assays are received and QAQC’d, the Company plans to provide an updated MRE, scheduled for first half of 2027, which will form the basis of a feasibility study targeted for release in late 2027.”

 

 

Q3-2026 Results

 

In Q3-2026, Contango’s share of production sold from the Manh Choh mine, jointly held by Kinross and Contango, totaled 7,333 ounces of gold and 5,349 ounces of silver. Contango received a cash distribution of $6 million (“M”) from the Peak Gold JV on October 7, 2026 relating to Q3-2026 production. On a consolidated basis in Q3, the Company reported a total loss from operations of $24.2 M, net loss of $23.0 M and an adjusted net loss1 of $20.9 M, primarily related to expensed exploration across all projects. The Company's cash and cash equivalents as of September 30, 2026 was $56.6 M compared to $64.8 M as of December 31, 2025.

Manh Choh Mine:

 

On October 7, 2026, Peak Gold JV delivered a $6 M cash distribution to Contango, bringing the total cash distributions to $24 M for the first three campaigns in 2026. In total, Contango has received $166.5 M in

1 See non-GAAP measures in the Adjusted Net Income/(Loss) (Non-GAAP) section of this press release for calculation of Adjusted Net Income

 

cash distributions from the Peak JV since mining operations commenced in July 2024. The focus for 2026 has been transitioning from the north pit to the south pit, resulting in higher mining costs associated with pre-stripping. In Q3 ore tons mined at Manh Choh increased approximately 16% to 304,000 tons vs Q2. Reduced mining equipment availability early in Q2 and Q3 has been addressed with additional drilling, loading and haulage equipment added on site.

 

Gold production for the quarter came in below budget due to several factors including:

 

•
The third milling campaign of 2026 ended earlier than planned due to persistent above average rainy conditions in September, resulting in fewer gold ounces being processed during the quarter - the remaining planned ounces being deferred until the fourth campaign of 2026
•
Tons processed at the Fort Knox mill were additionally reduced in Q3 due to mine sequencing and changes in ore characteristics at Manh Choh which resulted in increased downtime for mill maintenance and modifications to handle the higher copper and sulfide content

 

In Q3-2026 gold recovery at the mill was 86.5%, improving from 80.4% in Q2-2026, as the south pit contains much higher copper and sulfide content. During Q3-2026, improvements were made with the installation of a new oxygen sparging system, the restoration of a cement addition system and upgrades to the silo cone and screw. In addition, gravity projects were completed to stabilize the gravity recovery average. The focus continues on effective pre-aeration, sulfide binding with cement and cyanide destruction, which includes adding more air sparging to detox reactors. Further work is planned for oxygen generators and a bypass tails wash thickener to reduce cyanide reporting to the grind circuit.

 

Ore transportation from the Manh Choh mine to the Fort Knox milling facility continues to outperform and is expected to exceed our planned budget for 2026.

 

The cash costs and AISC for Q3-2026 were $2,699 and $2,909 per ounce, respectively. For the first nine months of 2026, the cash costs and AISC were $2,676 and $2,854, respectively, primarily driven by reduced gold ounces processed and sold. A slight increase in direct operating costs in 2026 is primarily driven by increased diesel costs related to ore transportation from Manh Choh to Fort Knox and increased reagents and consumables required in the mill to process the higher copper and sulfide content.

The fourth and final campaign for 2026 is expected to produce 13,000 to 15,000 ounces of gold equivalent ounces at cash costs and AISC of $2,700 and $2,900, respectively. The Company is guiding towards the lower end of its 2026 production guidance of 40,000 gold equivalent ounces attributable to its 30% interest in the Peak Gold JV with 2026 cash costs and AISC of $2,700 and $2,900 per ounce of gold sold2, respectively. A final cash distribution in the amount of $16 M is expected in the fourth quarter for a total of $40 M in cash distributions to be received for 2026.

 

Lucky Shot Project:

Underground Drilling & Development: The initial underground drilling phase comprised 65 HQ diamond holes totaling approximately 6,020 meters. Further surface drilling, underground access and KM vein drilling are intended to support an updated resource estimate targeted for the first half of 2027, followed by feasibility evaluation in late 2027. Underground exploration-access work has resumed with contractor on site. The Company remains focused on advancing a high-grade Direct Shipping Ore (DSO) operation

targeting 40,000 to 50,000 gold ounces per year, subject to technical results, permitting and a future production decision.

Surface Drilling Completed: The surface program comprised approximately 5,800 meters in 27 holes from five pads and was completed on August 31, 2026. Results announced on September 2, 2026 included LSS26016 with 2.45 meters grading 86.05 g/t Au. Subsequent to quarter end, results announced on October 6, 2026 included LSS26018 with 0.63 meters grading 140.23 g/t Au and LSS26021 with 3.65 meters grading 18.16 g/t Au. These are selected downhole intervals and true widths have not been established. Additional assays remain pending.

KM Vein Drilling: KM vein drilling began on September 16, 2026. As of the October 6, 2026 project update, the program comprised approximately 1,140 meters in 17 planned holes from five stations, with ten holes completed from the first two stations. The program is evaluating the geometry of the KM vein and its interpreted structural relationship to the Lucky Shot Fault and vein system. Assays are expected in the fourth quarter of 2026.

Kitsault Valley Project:

 

Expanded Drill Campaign: The 2026 drilling program was expanded from 40,000 meters to more than 56,000 meters. Results announced on September 15, 2026 included DV26-480 with 13.90 meters grading 506 g/t AgEq and DV26-494 with 4.96 meters grading 807 g/t AgEq. Intervening holes showed mineralization consistent with a possible geological connection between Torbrit and North Star; assays were pending and continuity of grade has not been established. Intervals are downhole lengths and true widths require further validation. Silver equivalent grades are calculated using the metal prices and recovery assumptions set out in the Company’s September 15, 2026 news release.

 

Updated Mineral Resource Estimate: On September 11, 2026, the Company reported an updated MRE for the Kitsault Valley Project comprising 7.66 million tonnes of indicated resources containing 58.70 million ounces of silver and 394.70 thousand ounces of gold (89.55 million ounces AgEq), and approximately 6.31 million tonnes of inferred resources containing 22.80 million ounces of silver and 620.82 thousand ounces of gold (64.92 million ounces AgEq). The MRE is based on drilling completed through 2025 and excludes 2026 drilling. The MRE is supported by a technical report summary filed with the Company’s Form 8-K on September 22, 2026. In H1-2027 the Company is planning to update the MRE with the drilling completed in 2026, which will form the basis of the IA targeted for release in late 2027.

 

 

 

 

Johnson Tract Project:

Road & Portal Access: During the third quarter, the Company completed construction of the 2.6-mile access road connecting the existing camp to the proposed portal site, along with the portal laydown area. This work supports construction of the underground exploration drift, which is scheduled to begin in 2027.

Permitting & Site Preparation: Environmental and cultural surveys within the Johnson Tract and project easements were largely completed during the season and the baseline program is substantially advanced. Geotechnical investigations within the foreshore have been completed and investigations along the proposed coastal road are substantially complete. The project continues to advance under the FAST-41

 

permitting timetable and remains on schedule for underground exploration tunnel construction to begin in 2027.

Section 404 Permit Upheld: On September 14, 2026, the U.S. District Court for the District of Alaska ruled in favor of JT Mining in the lawsuit challenging the Johnson Tract Section 404 permit. Final judgment in favor of JT Mining was entered on September 15, 2026, and the permit remains in place.

Statement of Operations for Q3-2026 compared to Q3-2025:

 

The Company reported a loss from operations of $24.2 M in Q3-2026 compared to income from operations of $25.0 M in Q3-2025. The change was primarily due to increased exploration expenditures of $26.9 M (Q3-2025: $2.1 M) and lower income from the Peak Gold JV of $6.9 M (Q3-2025: $29.5 M) during the quarter. The higher exploration expense includes drilling at the Kitsault Valley Project, which was added through the Dolly Varden acquisition in Q1-2026 and therefore has no comparable costs in Q3-2025, as well as expanded drilling and underground development at Lucky Shot and permitting and road work at Johnson Tract.

 

The Company reported a net loss of $23.0 M, or $0.66 per fully diluted share, compared to a net loss of $5.4 M, or $0.44 per fully diluted share, in Q3-2025. The net loss includes losses on derivative contracts of $2.0 M in Q3-2026 and $30.3 M in Q3-2025. The increase in net loss was mainly due to higher exploration expense, including at the Kitsault Valley Project (acquired in Q1-2026), and lower income from the Peak Gold JV, partly offset by lower losses on derivative contracts. Excluding these losses, the Company reported an adjusted net loss of $20.9 M in Q3-2026 compared to adjusted net income of $24.9 M in Q3-2025.

 

Gold equivalent ounces produced and sold in the third quarter of 2026, both decreased by 56% compared to the third quarter of 2025, primarily due to the decrease in tons processed from Manh Choh as the project transitions from the north pit to the south pit.

 

Statement of Cash Flows for YTD-2026 compared to YTD-2025:

 

Net cash used in operating activities was $109.1 M for YTD-2026 compared to $60.2 M provided by operating activities in YTD-2025. The change was primarily driven by cash paid to settle hedge contracts ($97.7 M in 2026 compared to $26.7 M in 2025) and lower cash distributions received from the Peak Gold JV ($18.0 M in 2026 compared to $87.0 M in 2025) during YTD-2026. Cash provided by investing activities was $7.4 M for YTD-2026 compared to cash used of $0.4 M in YTD-2025, primarily reflecting $36.0 M of cash acquired in the Dolly Varden acquisition, partially offset by $21.4 M of property and equipment additions, which included the Johnson Tract access road and the purchase of the Lucky Shot underlying lease and 2% net smelter return royalty, and $7.2 M of acquisition transaction costs related to Dolly Varden. Cash provided by financing activities in YTD-2026 was $94.1 M, primarily related to net proceeds from equity issuances of $63.4 M and $33.7 M of proceeds from the credit facility amendment, partially offset by principal repayments of $3.0 M on the credit facility. This compares to cash provided by financing activities of $26.9 M in YTD-2025, primarily related to net proceeds from equity issuances of $56.6 M, partially offset by principal repayments of $29.0 M on the credit facility. The Company’s cash and cash equivalents position as of September 30, 2026 was $56.6 M compared to $64.8 M as of December 31, 2025.

 

Gold equivalent ounces produced and sold in the first nine months of 2026 both decreased by 53% compared to the first nine months of 2025, primarily due to a decrease in tons processed and planned mill grades from Manh Choh as the project transitions from the north pit to the south pit.

ABOUT CONTANGO

Contango is an NYSE American and TSX-listed mining company that engages in the exploration for and development of silver, gold, and associated minerals with a growth strategy focused on district-scale silver and gold exploration in British Columbia’s Golden Triangle funded by high-grade gold production in Alaska. The Company's flagship Canadian asset comprises approximately 247,000 acres (100,000 hectares) of prospective silver-gold mineral tenures in and around the Kitsault Valley, the southern cornerstone of the Golden Triangle. In Alaska, Contango holds a 30% interest in the Peak Gold JV, which leases approximately 675,000 acres of land for production and exploration on the Manh Choh project, with the remaining 70% owned by KG Mining (Alaska), Inc., an indirect subsidiary of Kinross Gold Corporation, operator of the Peak Gold JV. The Company and its subsidiaries also hold: (i) a lease on the Johnson Tract project, which consists of mineral rights to approximately 21,000 acres located near tidewater, 125 miles southwest of Anchorage, Alaska, from the underlying owner, CIRI; (ii) 100% ownership of the Lucky Shot project, which consists of mineral rights to approximately 8,600 acres of State of Alaska and patented mining claims located in the Willow Mining District about 75 miles north of Anchorage, Alaska; (iii) mineral rights to approximately 145,000 acres of State of Alaska mining claims; and (iv) mineral rights to approximately 15,260 acres of State of Alaska mining claims and upland mining leases, all of which give Contango the exclusive right to explore and develop minerals on these lands.
