Legence Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Record Quarterly Revenues of $1.31 Billion, an 85% Increase from a Year Ago

Excluding Bowers Acquisition, Revenues (non-GAAP) Grew by 40% from a Year Ago1

Quarterly Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) Increased 99% from Prior Year2

Record Total Backlog and Awarded Contracts of $5.90 Billion, a 92% Increase from a Year Ago

Board Authorizes $150 Million Stock Repurchase Program

Establish Fourth Quarter 2026 Guidance for Revenue of $1.26 Billion - $1.30 Billion and Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA of $160 Million - $170 Million

Raise Full Year 2026 Guidance for Revenue to $4.87 Billion - $4.91 Billion and Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA of $610 Million - $620 Million

Establish Full Year 2027 Guidance for Revenue of $5.3 Billion - $5.6 Billion and Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA of $690 Million - $730 Million

SAN JOSE, California – November 12, 2026 – Legence Corp. (Nasdaq: LGN) (“Legence” or the “Company”) today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“Strong customer demand drove record third-quarter revenue, Adjusted EBITDA, and backlog and awarded contracts for Legence,” said Jeff Sprau, Chief Executive Officer of Legence. “Our team continues to deliver exceptional project execution, which along with an increasing mix of fabrication work, contributed to the delivery of the highest Adjusted EBITDA margin in company history. Additionally, we also recently acquired a mechanical contractor in central Texas, creating an ideal platform to expand customer support across this high-growth region. Our confidence in the fundamentals of our business is reflected in our increased guidance for this year, and growth embedded in our initial 2027 guidance. This outlook also underpins the Board’s authorization of our $150 million stock repurchase program, which broadens our capital allocation options.”

Third Quarter 2026 Consolidated Results:

Revenues for the third quarter 2026 totaled $1.31 billion, an increase of 85.1% from $708.0 million for the third quarter 2025. Excluding the impact of the acquisition of The Bowers Group, Inc. (“Bowers”), non-GAAP revenue growth was 39.5%. Gross profit for the third quarter 2026 was $275.5 million with gross margin of 21.0%, compared to gross profit of $148.1 million and gross margin of 20.9% for the third quarter 2025. Excluding the impact of compensation related to legacy Series A Interests and Restricted Series C Interests paid for by entities outside of Legence, we generated non-GAAP Adjusted Gross Profit of $266.6 million and non-GAAP Adjusted Gross Margin of 20.3% for the third quarter 2026, compared to non-GAAP Adjusted Gross Profit of $151.9 million and non-GAAP Adjusted Gross Margin of 21.5% for the third quarter 2025. The decrease in non-GAAP Adjusted Gross Margin was primarily due to a revenue mix shift towards Installation & Maintenance. Net income attributable to Legence for the third quarter 2026 was $89.6 million, or $1.15 per diluted share, compared to a net loss attributable to Legence of $0.6 million for the third quarter 2025, or $(0.02) per diluted share. Net income for the third quarter 2026 was $133.9 million, compared to a net income of $0.4 million for the third quarter 2025. Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter 2026 was $177.0 million, an increase of 99.3% from $88.8 million for the third quarter 2025. Refer to “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” for definitions of revenue growth (excluding Bowers), Adjusted Gross Profit, Adjusted Gross Margin, Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA Margin and a reconciliation of each to the most directly comparable GAAP measure.

Engineering & Consulting Segment Results:

Engineering & Consulting segment revenue for the third quarter 2026 totaled $199.9 million, a decrease of (5.8)% from $212.2 million for the third quarter 2025, driven by a decline in Program & Project Management services and modestly lower Engineering & Design revenue, primarily from education clients.

Engineering & Consulting segment gross profit for the third quarter 2026 totaled $71.7 million, an increase of 6.5% from $67.3 million for the third quarter 2025. Excluding the impact of compensation related to legacy Series A Interests and Restricted Series C Interests paid for by entities outside of Legence, we generated non-GAAP Adjusted Gross Profit of $66.6 million and non-GAAP Adjusted Gross Margin of 33.3% for the third quarter 2026, compared to non-GAAP Adjusted Gross Profit of $69.6 million and non-GAAP Adjusted Gross Margin of 32.8% for the third quarter 2025. Refer to “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” for definitions of Adjusted Gross Profit and Adjusted Gross Margin and a reconciliation of each to the most directly comparable GAAP measure. The decrease in non-GAAP Adjusted Gross Profit was primarily driven by lower revenue, partially offset by higher non-GAAP Adjusted Gross Margin. The increase in non-GAAP Adjusted Gross Margin was primarily driven by higher project margins in our Program & Project Management service line, and a favorable revenue mix shift toward Engineering & Design.

Installation & Maintenance Segment Results:

Installation & Maintenance segment revenue for the third quarter 2026 totaled $1.11 billion, an increase of 124.0% from $495.8 million for the third quarter 2025. Excluding the impact of the Bowers acquisition, non-GAAP Installation & Maintenance segment revenues grew by 58.9% over the comparable period.3 The increase was driven by strong demand for our Installation & Fabrication services, primarily from data centers & technology clients. The increase in Maintenance & Service revenue was primarily driven by the acquisition of Bowers and growth from education and other clients. Backlog and Awarded Contracts and Book-to-Bill Ratio

Backlog and awarded contracts totaled $5.90 billion at September 30, 2026, an increase of 92.5% from $3.07 billion at September 30, 2025. The consolidated book-to-bill ratio for the three-month period ended September 30, 2026 was 1.2x. Engineering & Consulting segment backlog and awarded contracts increased by 32.6% year over year, primarily from growth in the education and state & local government end markets. Installation & Maintenance segment backlog and awarded contracts increased by 117.2% year over year, primarily from the acquisition of Bowers and strong growth in the data centers & technology and education end markets.

Acquisitions

On November 1, 2026, the Company completed the acquisition of Central Texas Industrial Contractors (“CTI”). Founded in 2010, CTI is an Austin, Texas-based mechanical contractor supporting mission-critical infrastructure, with a strong presence in the semiconductor end market in central Texas. “Texas is a strategic geographic region for Legence. With CTI, we immediately establish a reputable platform to expand our business and support our customers in this region,” said Jeff Sprau, Chief Executive Officer of Legence. “CTI also adds a high-quality list of new customers and shares a strong cultural alignment with Legence. We are excited to welcome CTI to our team, and look forward to expanding our service reach and accelerating our growth together in this geography.” For the calendar year 2026, CTI is expected to generate revenue of approximately $40 million, of which Legence expects to record approximately $6 to $8 million of revenue during the fourth quarter of 2026.

Share Repurchase Plan

In early November 2026, the Company’s Board of Directors authorized a $150 million share repurchase plan, reflecting the Company’s strong financial position and outlook, as well as a commitment to a disciplined approach to capital allocation. The new share repurchase plan will be executed in accordance with applicable securities laws and regulations, does not require any specific number of shares to be acquired, and may be suspended or discontinued at any time at the Company’s discretion.

Balance Sheet

At September 30, 2026, the Company had cash and cash equivalents of approximately $340.8 million and total debt4 of approximately $1.02 billion. As a result, net leverage was 1.3 times, based on non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA of the Company for the last 12 months ended September 30, 2026 (“Legence LTM adjusted EBITDA”). When including non-GAAP EBITDA of Bowers for the three months ended December 31, 2025 together with Legence LTM adjusted EBITDA, adjusted net leverage was 1.2 times. About Legence

Legence is a leading provider of engineering, consulting, installation, and maintenance services for mission-critical systems in buildings. The Company specializes in designing, fabricating, and installing complex HVAC, process piping, and other mechanical, electrical and plumbing (MEP) systems—enhancing energy efficiency, reliability, and sustainability in new and existing facilities. Legence also delivers long-term performance through strategic upgrades and holistic solutions. Serving some of the world’s most technically demanding sectors, Legence counts over 60% of the Nasdaq-100 Index among its clients.

Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA Margin; Net Leverage and Adjusted Net Leverage

Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA Margin are financial measures not presented in accordance with GAAP but are intended to provide useful and supplemental information to investors and analysts as they evaluate our performance. Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income (loss) adjusted to exclude, or otherwise reflect, interest expense, interest income, income tax expense, depreciation and amortization, credit agreement amendment fees,

 

12

goodwill impairment, long-lived asset impairment, net (gain) loss on sale and disposition of property and equipment, loss on debt extinguishment, acquisition and integration costs, system deployment costs, strategic initiative costs, indemnification asset adjustments, Tax Receivable Agreement liability remeasurements and stock-based and other non-cash compensation expense (benefit). Adjusted EBITDA Margin is defined as Adjusted EBITDA divided by revenue. Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA Margin should not be considered alternatives to net income (loss) or net income (loss) margin, respectively, as determined in accordance with GAAP. Management believes that the exclusion of the above-described items from net income (loss) in the presentation of the non-GAAP measures identified above enables us and our investors to more effectively evaluate our operations period over period and to identify operating trends that might not be apparent due to, among other reasons, the variable nature of these items, both in value and frequency, period over period. In addition, management believes these measures may be useful for investors in comparing our operating results with those of other companies.

Net leverage is defined as net debt of Legence divided by Adjusted EBITDA of Legence, and adjusted net leverage is defined as net debt of Legence divided by LTM combined adjusted EBITDA. Backlog and Awarded Contracts and Book-to-Bill Ratio

We believe that backlog and awarded contracts and book-to-bill ratio enable us to more effectively forecast our future results and working capital needs, as well as better identify future operating trends that may not otherwise be apparent. Backlog represents, as of any date of determination, the expected revenue values of the remaining performance obligations under our contracted fixed-price projects. Awarded contracts represents, as of any date of determination, the expected revenue values of projects awarded to us following a request for proposals but for which a formal contract has not yet been signed. We calculate our book-to-bill ratio by taking our additions to backlog and awarded contracts, excluding additions that were attained through acquisition, for the period, and dividing it by revenue from fixed-price contracts for the same period. Given that backlog and awarded contracts and book-to-bill ratio are operational measures and that our methodology for calculating each such measure does not meet the definition of a non-GAAP financial measure, as that term is defined by the SEC, a quantitative reconciliation for each is not required or provided.
