iBio Reports Q1 Fiscal Year 2027 Financial Results and Provides Corporate Update

Presented additional preclinical data supporting IBIO-610’s potential to drive fat selective weight loss in combination with GLP-1 therapy

Completed dosing in the single ascending dose portion of the Phase 1 clinical trial of IBIO-600

Plans to participate in upcoming scientific and investor conferences

SAN DIEGO, November 10, 2026 -- iBio, Inc. (NASDAQ: IBIO), a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing long-acting antibody therapeutics for obesity, cardiometabolic and cardiopulmonary diseases, today announced its financial results for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027, ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update on its progress.

“This was a quarter of focus and execution as we advanced our portfolio of long-acting antibody therapeutics designed to address important challenges in obesity care,” said Martin Brenner, DVM, Ph.D., Chief Executive Officer and Chief Scientific Officer of iBio. “The data presented at EASD strengthen our conviction in IBIO-610’s potential to deliver high-quality weight loss and provide important preclinical validation as we advance toward an IND-equivalent filing by the end of 2026, with plans to initiate first-in-human studies in the first half of 2027. We also anticipate advancing IBIO-600 in the multiple ascending dose portion of the Phase 1 trial in the first quarter of 2027 and progressing our preclinical programs that aim to address the entire spectrum of cardiometabolic disease care. Together, this progress brings us closer to our broader goal of helping people living with obesity achieve high-quality weight loss and better long-term health.”

First Fiscal Quarter 2027 & Recent Corporate Updates

·

Presented additional preclinical body composition data from a study of IBIO-610, iBio’s long-acting Activin E antibody, in obese non-human primates (NHPs) at the European Association for the Study of Diabetes (EASD) Annual Meeting on October 1st.

o

At week eight, fat accounted for 93% of the weight lost by NHPs receiving IBIO-610 in combination with semaglutide, compared with 77% for NHPs receiving semaglutide alone. Lean mass accounted for 6% and 22% of weight lost, respectively.

o

These preclinical findings further support the potential of IBIO-610 to improve body composition, both as a standalone therapy and in combination with GLP-1 receptor agonists.

o

iBio expects to initiate first-in-human trials of IBIO-610 in the first half of 2027.

·

Completed dosing in the single ascending dose (SAD) portion of the first-in-human Phase 1 clinical trial of IBIO-600, iBio’s long-acting anti-myostatin antibody being developed to preserve muscle and improve body composition.

o

iBio anticipates interim Phase 1 data of the SAD study in the first quarter of 2027.

o

The multiple ascending dose portion of the Phase 1 clinical trial is anticipated to start in the first quarter of 2027.

·

Plans to participate in upcoming scientific and investor conferences, including:

Financial Results

·

Revenues for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were approximately $1.1 million, an increase of $1.0 over the $0.1 million in revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

·

Research and Development (“R&D”) expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 were approximately $6.7 million and $3.6 million, respectively, an increase of approximately $3.1 million or approximately 87%. The increase in R&D expenses is primarily due to increased spending of approximately $2.6 million for consultants and outside services supporting our R&D efforts, including the IBIO-600 Phase 1 human clinical trial, NHP studies and CMC activities, and an increase of approximately $0.3 million in personnel costs as a result of advancing research activities for our IBIO-600, IBIO-610, and IBIO-800 programs and other preclinical pipeline assets.

·

General and Administrative expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and September 30, 2025, were approximately $2.8 million and $2.5 million, respectively, an increase of approximately $0.3 million or approximately 10%. The increase is primarily attributable to an increase in personnel costs and legal fees.

·

iBio held cash and cash equivalents and investments in debt securities of approximately $76.7 million as of September 30, 2026.

About iBio, Inc.

iBio (Nasdaq: IBIO) is a clinical-stage biotechnology developing long-acting antibody therapeutics for obesity, cardiometabolic and cardiopulmonary diseases, cancer, and other hard-to-treat diseases. Combining advanced antibody engineering with AI-driven discovery, iBio is advancing a differentiated pipeline designed to deliver sustained therapeutic effects and address significant unmet medical needs. iBio’s mission is to transform drug discovery, accelerate development timelines, and unlock new possibilities in precision medicine.
