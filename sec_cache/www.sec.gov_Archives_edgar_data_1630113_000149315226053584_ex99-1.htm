Biotricity Reports Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Results

 

Revenue up 11.5%, gross margin 83.3% and income from operations up 23.7%.

 

REDWOOD CITY, CA / November 20, 2026 / Biotricity Inc. (OTCQB: BTCY) (“Biotricity” or the “Company”), a medical technology company providing remote cardiac monitoring solutions, announced its financial results for the second quarter of fiscal 2027, ended September 30, 2026.

 

Dr. Waqaas Al-Siddiq, Biotricity Founder and CEO, said, “Our second quarter delivered year-over-year growth in technology fee revenue, a higher gross margin and operating leverage. Revenue grew 11.5%, gross margin expanded to 83.3% and income from operations rose 23.7%, as gross profit growth outpaced higher operating expenses, including the timing of research and development activities.”

 

Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Highlights (compared to the second quarter of fiscal 2026)

 

●Revenue increased 11.5% to $4.33 million. Technology fee revenue, the majority of which is recurring, increased 13.3% to $3.98 million, or 91.8% of total revenue (prior-year quarter: 90.4%).
   
●Gross margin improved to 83.3% from 81.9%, driven by technology fee gross margin of 85.5% (prior-year quarter: 82.3%). Gross profit increased 13.4% to $3.61 million.
   
●Operating expenses increased 12.5% to $3.27 million, primarily due to a 46.7% increase in research and development expenses to $0.88 million; selling, general and administrative expenses increased 3.5% from increased sales. Income from operations increased 23.7% to $339 thousand, an operating margin of 7.8% (prior-year quarter: 7.0%).
   
●Net loss before dividends was $0.81 million (prior-year quarter: $0.69 million), including a $151 thousand non-cash loss on the change in fair value of derivative liabilities. Net loss attributable to common stockholders was $0.95 million, or $(0.046) per share, compared to $0.77 million, or $(0.029) per share, on fewer weighted average shares.

 

Six-Month Highlights (compared to the prior-year period)

 

●Revenue increased 11.0% to $8.61 million, driven by an 18.3% increase in technology fee revenue. Gross margin improved to 82.9% from 81.2%.
   
●Net loss before dividends was $1.77 million (prior-year period: $1.36 million). Net loss attributable to common stockholders was $2.02 million, or $(0.092) per share, compared to $1.53 million, or $(0.057) per share.
   
●Net cash provided by operating activities was $511 thousand (prior-year period: $42 thousand), primarily reflecting higher accounts payable, accrued liabilities and collections of accounts receivable.

 

Corporate Developments

 

●The Company signed a national distribution agreement, subject to a successful pilot program, where the partner will fund commercial expansion in exchange for a 30% distribution margin. The 18-month pilot was officially kicked off in October and is restricted to 6 states. Upon a successful pilot, the program will be expanded.
   
●In October 2026, the Company was selected to provide cardiac screenings to patients in low-income communities and developing countries under a charitably funded program. The Company will be paid in advance at its standard commercial rates and currently expects the program to involve the deployment of at least 1,000 devices; there can be no assurance as to the timing or extent of deployment or the revenue ultimately recognized.
   
●Successfully established a long-term inventory financing program to fund production.

 

 

 

 

Full results are included in the Company’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, filed on November 16, 2026 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission and available at www.sec.gov.
