Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2026 Financial Highlights
▪Total revenue increased 50.3% to $74.1 million in Q3 2026 versus $49.3 million in the prior year period driven by Clinical acquisitions partially offset by Logistics. On a pro forma(1) basis, revenue increased 8.5% year-over-year in Q3 2026.
▪Logistics revenue decreased 3.5% to $44.9 million in Q3 2026 versus $46.5 million in the prior year period. On a sequential basis, Logistics revenue decreased 6.9%, towards the high end of our guidance framework and consistent with seasonal transplant industry volume declines.
▪Clinical revenue increased to $29.2 million in Q3 2026 versus $2.8 million in the prior year period driven by a full quarter contribution from the Keystone acquisition that closed in Q3 2025 and three acquisitions that closed in Q2 2026. Clinical revenue increased 20.3% sequentially in Q3 2026, driven primarily by the full quarter impact of recent acquisitions as well as modest organic sequential growth.
▪Gross profit increased 57.9% to $16.8 million in Q3 2026 versus $10.7 million in the prior year period driven by growth in Clinical and Clinical acquisitions partially offset by Logistics.
▪Gross margin increased 110 basis points to 22.7% in Q3 2026 versus 21.6% in the prior year period driven primarily by the positive mix impact of our larger Clinical segment, which also saw margin expansion versus 2025, partially offset by a decline in Logistics gross margin.
▪Logistics gross profit decreased 6.6% to $9.3 million in Q3 2026 versus $10.0 million in the prior year period. Logistics gross margin of 20.8% in Q3 2026 declined versus 21.5% the prior year period driven primarily by an increase in fuel surcharge pass through revenue, and a reduction in average distances between donor and recipient, industry-wide and at Strata, which results in lower margins on owned aircraft. Logistics gross margin rebounded 240 basis points sequentially to 20.8% from 18.4% in Q2 2026.
▪Clinical gross profit increased to $7.5 million in Q3 2026 versus $0.7 million in Q3 2025, driven primarily by a full quarter contribution from the Keystone acquisition that closed in Q3 2025 and three acquisitions that closed in Q2 2026. Clinical gross margin increased to 25.6% in Q3 2026 versus 23.8% in Q3 2025. Clinical gross margin declined 50 basis points versus 26.1% in Q2 2026 primarily due to investments in staff and training to support future growth.
▪Total Selling, General and Administrative expenses decreased $0.8 million to $15.1 million in Q3 2026 versus $15.9 million in Q3 2025. Adjusted SG&A(1) increased $1.5 million to $8.9 million in Q3 2026 versus $7.5 million in Q3 2025 driven primarily by the acquisitions in Clinical. On a sequential basis, Adjusted SG&A(1) decreased $0.2 million in Q3 2026 versus Q2 2026.
▪Net loss from continuing operations improved by $2.0 million to $(7.6) million in Q3 2026 versus $(9.7) million in Q3 2025.
▪Adjusted EBITDA(1) was $9.6 million in Q3 2026 versus $4.2 million in Q3 2025. Adjusted EBITDA margin rose to 13.0% in Q3 2026 versus 8.5% in Q3 2025. The 450 basis points increase in Adjusted EBITDA margin versus Q3 2025 was driven by the increase in Clinical gross margin and a reduction in Adjusted SG&A as a percentage of revenue, partially offset by a decrease in Logistics gross margin.
▪Cash flow from operating activities was $9.5 million in Q3 2026.
▪Free Cash Flow was $8.6 million in Q3 2026.
▪Ended Q3 2026 with $41.6 million in cash and short-term investments.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Information

Strata believes that the non-GAAP measures discussed below, viewed in addition to and not in lieu of our reported U.S. generally accepted accounting principles ("GAAP") results, provide useful information to investors by providing a more focused measure of operating results, enhance the overall understanding of past financial performance and future prospects, and allow for greater transparency with respect to key metrics used by management in its financial and operational decision making. The non-GAAP measures presented herein may not be comparable to similarly titled measures presented by other companies. Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted SG&A, Free Cash Flow, Free Cash Flow before aircraft and engine acquisitions, and pro forma revenue have all been reconciled to the nearest GAAP measure in the tables within this press release.

Adjusted EBITDA – Strata reports Adjusted EBITDA, which is a non-GAAP financial measure. Strata defines Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) from continuing operations adjusted to exclude: (1) depreciation and amortization; (2) stock-based compensation; (3) change in fair value of warrant liabilities and other assets and liabilities; (4) interest income and expense; (5) income tax; (6) realized gains and losses on short-term investments; and (7) certain other non-recurring items that management does not believe are indicative of the Company's ongoing operating performance and would impact the comparability of results between periods.

Adjusted SG&A – Strata defines Adjusted selling, general and administrative ("SG&A") expenses as SG&A adjusted to exclude: (1) depreciation; (2) stock-based compensation; and (3) other non-cash items and certain other non-recurring items that management does not believe are indicative of the Company's ongoing operating performance that would impact the comparability of results between periods.

Free Cash Flow, and Free Cash Flow before aircraft and engine acquisitions – Strata defines Free Cash Flow as net cash provided by / (used in) operating activities less capital expenditures and capitalized software development costs (net of proceeds from disposals). Free Cash Flow before aircraft and engine acquisitions is defined as Free Cash Flow excluding cash outflows related to aircraft and engine acquisitions. Strata believes these measures provide valuable insights into the Company's cash-generating capacity. In particular, Free Cash Flow before aircraft and engine acquisitions highlights the cash generated by Strata's continuing operations prior to the impact of aircraft and engine acquisitions, which are discretionary and strategic in nature.

Pro forma revenue – Strata defines pro forma revenue as reported revenue adjusted to include the revenue of the OVPA, LPS and HLT-NRP acquisitions and the Keystone acquisition as if each had occurred at the beginning of the annual period preceding the year of acquisition. Strata believes this measure is useful to investors because it facilitates the evaluation of period-over-period revenue growth of the combined business on a comparable basis. Pro forma revenue is presented for informational purposes only and is not necessarily indicative of the results that would have been achieved had the acquisitions occurred on those dates or of future results.

About Strata Critical Medical
Strata is a time-critical logistics and medical services provider to the U.S. healthcare industry. We operate one of the nation’s largest air transport and surgical services networks for transplant hospitals and organ procurement organizations, offering an integrated “one call” solution for donor organ recovery.
Strata’s core services include air and ground logistics, surgical organ recovery, organ placement and normothermic regional perfusion for the transplant industry, as well as perfusion staffing and equipment solutions for cardiovascular surgery centers.

For more information, visit www.srta.com.
