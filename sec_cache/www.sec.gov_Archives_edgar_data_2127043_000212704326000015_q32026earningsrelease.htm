Jersey Mike’s Reports Third Quarter Financial Results

Company updates full-year 2026 outlook
Announces $100 million share repurchase authorization
Declares $0.35 special dividend to holders of Class A common stock
Tinton Falls, NJ - October 28, 2026 — Jersey Mike’s Subs Inc. (NYSE: JMKE) today announced financial results for the fiscal third quarter ended September 27, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights:
•Same-store sales increased 3.0%, primarily driven by transaction growth
•Opened 80 new stores, driving net store growth of 8.8% year over year
•Systemwide sales increased 11% year over year to $1.204 billion
•Total revenue increased 10% year over year to $209 million
•Digital sales mix increased to 43% from 42% in the prior year
•Net income for the quarter of $10 million versus $32 million in the prior year
•Adjusted EBITDA for the quarter of $125 million versus $104 million in the prior year
•Diluted loss per share of Class A common stock of $0.05 for the period from July 31, 2026 through September 27, 2026
•Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted EPS of $74 million and $0.23, respectively
•Announced a return of capital of up to $200 million, including a share repurchase authorization of up to $100 million of Class A common stock and a special cash dividend of $0.35 per share of Class A common stock
“We continue to see meaningful traction from our digital marketing initiatives as we work to expand our consumer reach and drive greater frequency over time,” said Charlie Morrison, Chief Executive Officer. “Our same-store sales accelerated sequentially in the third quarter, with growth continuing to be driven by transactions for the third consecutive quarter. We remain focused on our long-term strategic goals of driving average unit volumes to $2 million, growing cash-on-cash returns for our franchise owners, and expanding our footprint domestically and around the world.”

Financial Results
Total revenue increased 10% year over year to $209 million. Royalties and other revenue increased 12% to $143 million and advertising revenue increased 8% to $57 million, both driven by net store growth of 8.8% and same-store sales growth of 3.0%, with the latter primarily driven by transactions.
Net income for the quarter was $10 million versus $32 million in the prior year, the decline being driven by a $29 million one-time, non-cash, equity-based compensation charge related to the IPO as well as IPO-related professional expenses, partially offset by a gain on the sale of company-owned stores. Net loss attributable to Jersey Mike’s Subs Inc. was $11 million, or $(0.05) per diluted share of Class A common stock for the period from July 31, 2026 through September 27, 2026 following the Reorganization Transactions and IPO.
Adjusted EBITDA increased 20% to $125 million versus $104 million in the prior year. The increase was driven by net store growth and growth in same-store sales, while also benefitting from $9 million in lower expenses related to the prior Area Director program.
Adjusted Net Income was $74 million and Adjusted EPS was $0.23 for the third quarter. Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted EPS, which the Company is presenting for the first time this quarter, assume the exchange of all outstanding Common Units for Class A common stock and reflect income taxes at a blended 25% rate as if Jersey Mike’s Subs Inc. owned 100% of Jersey Mike’s Holdings for the entire period. Adjusted EPS is not presented for the prior-year period because Jersey Mike’s pre-IPO capital structure does not provide a meaningful basis for comparison. See Table 5 for reconciliations to net income and diluted earnings per share, the most directly comparable GAAP measures.
The Company generated $177 million of operating cash flow in the first three fiscal quarters of 2026. Capital expenditures in the same period were $7 million. In connection with the issuance of 13,782,609 shares of Class A Common Stock, Jersey Mike’s received net proceeds of approximately $301 million, net of underwriting discounts and commissions, which were used to repay outstanding indebtedness. As of September 27, 2026, total debt was $1.8 billion and cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash were $325 million.

About Jersey Mike’s
Jersey Mike's is a leading fast-casual restaurant franchisor with more than 3,400 locations across the United States and Canada. Founded in 1956 as Mike's Subs in Point Pleasant, New Jersey, the company has grown from a single neighborhood sub shop into one of the fastest-growing restaurant brands in America. Jersey Mike's differentiates itself through its "A Sub Above" positioning, emphasizing fresh-sliced meats and cheeses, authentic recipes, and a distinctive customer experience that has earned recognition as the top-rated QSR brand in 2026 and the #1 Best Sandwich Chain in America in 2025.

Adjusted EBITDA - Defined as net income plus (i) interest expense, net of interest income; (ii) income tax expense; (iii) depreciation and amortization; (iv) equity-based compensation and related payroll taxes; (v) acquisition-related expenses; (vi) IPO-related expenses; (vii) founder-related discretionary expenses that by their nature have not recurred and are not expected to recur in periods following the Sponsor Acquisition; (viii) Area Director buyouts; (ix) corporate transition (severance, early contract termination, etc.) and other expenses; (x) loss on debt extinguishment; and (xi) the revaluation of the contingent liability; less (xii) gain on sale of company-owned stores. Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure.
Adjusted EPS - Defined as Adjusted Net Income divided by Adjusted Diluted Share Count. Adjusted EPS is a non-GAAP financial measure.
Adjusted G&A - Defined as general and administrative expenses excluding (i) equity-based compensation and related payroll taxes; (ii) acquisition-related expenses; (iii) IPO-related expenses; (iv) founder-related discretionary expenses; (v) Area Director buyouts; and (vi) corporate transition and other expenses, in each case to the extent included within general and administrative expenses. Adjusted G&A is a non-GAAP financial measure.
Adjusted Net Income - Defined as net income plus (i) income tax expense; (ii) amortization of acquired intangible assets; and (iii) the other items excluded from Adjusted EBITDA, except for interest expense, net and depreciation; less adjusted income tax expense, computed at an assumed blended effective tax rate of 25% applied to adjusted income before income taxes, as if Jersey Mike’s Subs Inc. owned 100% of Jersey Mike’s Holdings and were subject to corporate income taxes on all of its income for all periods presented. Adjusted Net Income is a non-GAAP financial measure.
Average Unit Volume (AUV) - Represents (i) the trailing 364 days sales of stores in the comparable store base, divided by (ii) the number of operating days of comparable stores in the same period, multiplied by (iii) 364. Operating days is equal to the number of days the store was open for business. The calculation of Average Unit Volume excludes both non-traditional stores and any traditional stores that have been open for less than 425 days.
Common Units, Incentive Units and Continuing Unitholders - Common Units refers to the common units of Jersey Mike’s Holdings, which, together with an equal number of shares of Class B common stock, are exchangeable for shares of Class A common stock on a one-for-one basis. Incentive Units refers to the incentive units of Jersey Mike’s Holdings, which have economic characteristics similar to stock appreciation rights and share in the equity value of Jersey Mike’s Holdings above specified participation thresholds, and which may be converted into Common Units based on that value. Continuing Unitholders refers collectively to the Continuing Common Unitholders and the Continuing Incentive Unitholders, the pre-IPO owners that continue to hold Common Units or Incentive Units, respectively, following the Reorganization Transactions.
Digital Sales Mix - Represents the percentage of Systemwide Sales that are generated through our digital channels (mobile app, online ordering, and third-party delivery) and measures the performance of our investments made in the digital platform and partnerships with third-party delivery partners.
IPO - Refers to the initial public offering of Class A common stock of Jersey Mike’s Subs Inc., which closed on July 31, 2026.

Terms and Definitions

Jersey Mike’s Holdings - Refers to Jersey Mike’s HoldCo, LLC, of which Jersey Mike’s Subs Inc. is the sole managing member following the Reorganization Transactions.
Net Store Growth - Represents (i) the total number of open stores as of a specific date divided by (ii) total number of open stores in the prior annual period, (iii) minus one.
New Store Openings - Represents the number of gross store openings in a period including franchised and company-owned stores.
Reorganization Transactions - Refers to the series of transactions completed in connection with the IPO, as described in our final prospectus, as a result of which Jersey Mike’s Subs Inc. became the sole managing member of, and consolidates, Jersey Mike’s Holdings.
Same-Store Sales Growth – Represents the change in year-over-year sales for the same store base on a constant-currency basis. We define the same-store base to include those traditional stores (whether company-owned or franchised) open for at least 425 days (14 calendar months). This measure highlights the performance of existing traditional stores, while excluding the impact of new traditional store openings and permanent closures. Same-Store Sales Growth is driven by increases in transactions and average check. Average check increases are driven by price increases or favorable mix shift from either an increase in items purchased or shifts into higher-priced items. Non-traditional stores, which are not included in Same-Store Sales Growth, include locations or operating models materially different than a standard Jersey Mike’s location, including kiosks, airports, colleges, commissaries, food courts, entertainment venues, etc., which make comparability year-over-year difficult or not meaningful.
Sponsor - Refers to investment funds associated with Blackstone Inc.
Sponsor Acquisition - Refers to the acquisition on January 16, 2025 by our Sponsor of a majority interest in Jersey Mike’s Holdings, including to indirectly hold 100% of the equity interests of Jersey Mike’s Franchise Systems, LLC.
Systemwide Sales – Represents net sales for all Jersey Mike’s stores. This measure allows management to better assess our overall store performance, the health of our brand and the strength of our market position compared to competitors. Our systemwide sales growth is driven by the number and sales volume of new store openings as well as Same-Store Sales Growth. Note that Systemwide Sales do not reflect our revenue and should not be viewed as a substitute for Total revenues discussed below.
Tax Receivable Agreement (“TRA”) - Refers to the tax receivable agreement we entered into in connection with the IPO, under which we are required to pay certain of our pre-IPO owners 90% of certain tax benefits, if any, that we actually realize.
Total Revenues - Reflects royalty and advertising revenue derived from Systemwide Sales across our franchised store base, supplemented by contributions from company-operated locations, as well as other revenues such as supplier program payments, upfront development and franchise fees, technology fees and gift card income.
Total Stores - Represents the number of stores in our system as of the relevant measurement date, including both company-owned and franchised stores and traditional and non-traditional stores.
