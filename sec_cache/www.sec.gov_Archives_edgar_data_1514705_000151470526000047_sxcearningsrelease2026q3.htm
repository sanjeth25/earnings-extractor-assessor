SUNCOKE ENERGY, INC. REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS


•Third quarter 2026 net income was $23.8 million, compared to $23.8 million in the prior year period; third quarter 2026 net income attributable to SXC was $21.8 million, or $0.25 per diluted share, compared to $22.2 million, or $0.26 per diluted share in the prior year period
•Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA(1) for the quarter was $76.9 million, compared to $59.1 million in the prior year period
•Reached a settlement agreement with Algoma Steel in October 2026 related to our 2025 claim
•Declared a cash dividend of $0.12 per share, representing the Company’s 29th consecutive quarterly dividend, payable on December 1, 2026
•Expect to modestly exceed the high-end of full-year 2026 Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA(1) guidance range of $250 million - $265 million

LISLE, Ill. (October 27, 2026) - SunCoke Energy, Inc. (NYSE: SXC) today reported results for the third quarter 2026, reflecting strong operational and financial performance.

"We are extremely pleased with the strong performance from our Industrial Services and Domestic Coke businesses in the third quarter," said Katherine Gates, President and Chief Executive Officer of SunCoke Energy, Inc. "Our Industrial Services segment benefited from higher terminals handling volumes and a full quarter of contribution from Phoenix, as compared to a partial quarter in the prior year period. Our Domestic Coke segment benefited from a change in mix of contract and spot coke sales and favorable coal-to-coke yields. Additionally, we are pleased to have reached a settlement agreement with Algoma Steel related to our 2025 claim." Gates continued, "Looking ahead, both segments continue to deliver strong and consistent operating performance, and we are well positioned to modestly exceed the high end of our full-year 2026 Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA guidance range of $250 million to $265 million."

Revenues in the third quarter of 2026 decreased $1.1 million as compared to the same prior year period, primarily driven by lower blast coke sales volumes due to the shutdown of our Haverhill I cokemaking facility and the pass-through of lower coal prices on our long-term, take-or-pay agreements, mostly offset by one additional month of Phoenix results and higher terminals handling volumes.

Net income attributable to SXC decreased $0.4 million as compared to the same prior year period, primarily driven by the absence of capital investment tax credits recorded in the third quarter of 2025, mostly offset by one additional month of Phoenix results, a change in mix of contract and spot coke sales, favorable coal-to-coke yields, property insurance recoveries from a 2023 Jewell flood event, and higher terminals handling volumes.

Adjusted EBITDA increased $17.8 million as compared to the same prior year period, primarily driven by one additional month of Phoenix results, a change in mix of contract and spot coke sales, favorable coal-to-coke yields, and higher terminals handling volumes, partially offset by lower coke sales volumes due to the shutdown of our Haverhill I cokemaking facility.

SUNCOKE ENERGY, INC.

SunCoke Energy, Inc. (NYSE: SXC) supplies high-quality coke to domestic and international customers. Our coke is used in the blast furnace production of steel as well as the foundry production of casted iron, with the majority of sales under long-term, take-or-pay contracts. We also export coke to overseas customers seeking high-quality product for their blast furnaces. Our process utilizes an innovative heat-recovery technology that captures excess heat for steam or electrical power generation and draws upon more than 60 years of cokemaking experience to operate our facilities in Illinois, Indiana, Ohio, Virginia and Brazil. Our industrial services business provides export and domestic material handling services to coke, coal, steel, power and other bulk customers, as well as mission-critical services to leading steel producers globally. The logistics terminals have the collective capacity to mix and transload more than 40 million tons of material each year and are strategically located to reach Gulf Coast, East Coast, Great Lakes and international ports. Additional industrial services include the removal, handling, and processing of molten slag at customer sites, as well as preparation and transportation of metal scraps, raw materials, and finished products. To learn more about SunCoke Energy, Inc., visit our website at www.suncoke.com.

DEFINITIONS
•Adjusted EBITDA represents earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization (“EBITDA”), adjusted for any impairments, restructuring costs, gains or losses on extinguishment of debt, gains or losses on foreign currency derivative instruments assumed as part of the acquisition of Phoenix Global, site closure costs and recoveries, property insurance recoveries, and/or transaction costs ("Adjusted EBITDA"). EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA do not represent and should not be considered alternatives to net income or operating income under U.S. GAAP and may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures in other businesses. Management believes Adjusted EBITDA is an important measure in assessing operating performance. Adjusted EBITDA provides useful information to investors because it highlights trends in our business that may not otherwise be apparent when relying solely on U.S. GAAP measures and because it eliminates items that have less bearing on our operating performance. EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA are not measures calculated in accordance with U.S. GAAP, and they should not be considered a substitute for net income, or any other measure of financial performance presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. Additionally, other companies may calculate Adjusted EBITDA differently than we do, limiting its usefulness as a comparative measure.
