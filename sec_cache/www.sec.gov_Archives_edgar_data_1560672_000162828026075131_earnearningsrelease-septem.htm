Ellington Credit Company Announces Financial Results for the Second Fiscal Quarter Ended September 30, 2026
OLD GREENWICH, Connecticut—November 19, 2026—Ellington Credit Company (NYSE: EARN) ("we") today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Highlights
•Net asset value (NAV) per share was $4.04 as of September 30, 2026, which includes the effects of distributions of $0.24 per share for the quarter.
•GAAP net income was $3.7 million, or $0.09 per share.
•Net investment income ("NII") was $6.7 million, or $0.17 per share.
◦Adjusted net investment income1 was $6.7 million, or $0.17 per share.
•CLO portfolio was $332.0 million as of September 30, 2026.
◦CLO debt investments—$146.4 million
◦CLO equity investments—$185.6 million
◦Purchased $41.5 million of CLO investments and sold $31.5 million, across 61 trades.
•Weighted average GAAP yield2 for the quarter, based on amortized cost, was 12.9% on the total CLO portfolio.
•Received $14.5 million in recurring cash distributions3 from the CLO portfolio, or $0.37 per share.
•Raised net proceeds of $5.5 million through the issuance of approximately 1.27 million common shares at prices representing a 3% weighted average premium to NAV per share.
•Subsequent to quarter end, reset our monthly distribution rate to $0.05 per share, effective with the distribution payable in January 2027.
Management Commentary
“Ellington Credit generated an economic return of 2.4% for the third calendar quarter, or 9.9% annualized, as our active trading and disciplined risk management helped us navigate renewed market volatility,” said Laurence Penn, Chief Executive Officer and President. “Our CLO mezzanine debt and equity portfolios both contributed positively, led by net investment income. Our credit hedges generated gains for the quarter and helped protect NAV per share as credit spreads widened in September. These positive contributions were partially offset by losses on interest rate hedges associated with our fixed-rate unsecured notes. We are proud to have continued our relative outperformance, generating positive GAAP earnings in a challenging quarter when the vast majority of our peers reported losses.
“We remained highly active during the quarter, executing 61 distinct CLO trades and continuing to rotate capital toward investments where we see more attractive risk-adjusted returns. Our overall portfolio size was roughly unchanged quarter over quarter, as sales, paydowns and deal calls largely offset new investment activity. Importantly, the weighted average yield on assets acquired during the quarter increased to 16.7%, up from 14.9% in the second quarter.
“Adjusted net investment income increased by $0.02 sequentially to $0.17 per share, and we continue to see opportunities for further increases through additional capital deployment, selective use of leverage, and continued portfolio rotation. Our Board of Trustees has reset our monthly distribution rate to $0.05 per share, or $0.15 per quarter, a level supported by our current NII. We believe that this positions us to maintain an attractive, sustainable distribution that is well covered by net investment income, while also building NAV per share.”

CLO Performance
During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Morningstar LSTA U.S. Leveraged Loan Index generated a total return of 1.9%, despite persistent credit dispersion and renewed volatility toward quarter end. Returns were supported by investor demand for floating-rate assets, a recovery in certain discounted loans, particularly in the software sector, and refinancings and maturity extensions. Double-B- and single-B-rated loans generated positive returns over the quarter, while triple-C-rated loans underperformed as rising interest rates pressured highly leveraged borrowers, particularly in cable and telecommunications. Loan prices declined in late September amid heightened inflation concerns and broader market volatility.
The European leveraged loan market underperformed the U.S. market, with more pronounced weakness in lower-quality credits. Headwinds included uncertainty over France’s fiscal and political outlook, energy-price volatility, and some forced selling from European credit funds.
CLO performance was mixed, as constructive loan-market technicals were tempered by the late-quarter selloff. Within CLO mezzanine debt, shorter-spread-duration, more seasoned positions benefited from stronger performance in shorter-maturity loans and principal paydowns that reduced deal-level leverage and strengthened structural credit protections. Meanwhile, higher-quality CLO equity positions with longer reinvestment periods benefited from stronger-than-anticipated interest payments in July, while limited loan repricing activity continued to support excess spread and equity cash flows.
However, higher borrowing costs pressured highly leveraged borrowers, weighing on CLO investments — particularly European CLO equity and lower-rated debt positions — with greater exposure to lower-quality collateral. In September, CLO tranche spreads widened modestly, and CLO equity valuations came under renewed pressure as broader markets weakened.
Our CLO mezzanine debt and equity portfolios each generated positive results for the quarter, as net investment income more than offset net realized and unrealized losses in our CLO equity investments. Credit hedges generated gains for the quarter overall, including in September, helping cushion the impact of that month’s spread widening on NAV per share. Additional headwinds included losses on interest rate hedges associated with our unsecured notes, and mark-to-market losses on the notes themselves as credit spread tightening more than offset the impact of higher interest rates.
We executed 61 distinct CLO trades during the quarter, with sales and paydowns largely offsetting new investment activity. The weighted average yield on assets acquired during the quarter increased to 16.7%, compared with 14.9% in the second quarter. At September 30, 2026, our CLO portfolio had a fair value of $332.0 million and a weighted average projected yield of 18.3% based on fair value.

About Ellington Credit Company
Ellington Credit Company (the "Fund") is a non-diversified closed-end fund that seeks to provide attractive current cash flow and risk-adjusted total returns by investing primarily in corporate collateralized loan obligations ("CLOs"), with a focus on mezzanine debt and equity tranches. The Fund is externally managed and advised by an affiliate of Ellington Management Group, L.L.C., a leading fixed-income investment manager founded in 1994. The Fund benefits from Ellington’s extensive experience and deep expertise in portfolio management, credit analysis, and risk management.

Reconciliation of Adjusted Net Investment Income to Net Investment Income
We calculate Adjusted Net Investment Income as net investment income adjusted for: (i) non-recurring expenses; (ii) non-capitalized transaction costs, (iii) net realized and change in net appreciation (depreciation) associated with periodic settlements on interest rate swaps, and (iv) interest and dividend income (expense) net of transaction fees associated with the referenced assets on total return swaps. Adjusted Net Investment Income is a supplemental non-GAAP financial measure. We believe that the presentation of Adjusted Net Investment Income provides information useful to investors, because: (i) we believe that it is a useful indicator of both current and projected long-term financial performance, in that it excludes the impact of certain expenses and non-capitalized transaction costs that we believe are less useful in forecasting long-term performance and distribution-paying ability; and (ii) we believe that realized and accrued periodic settlements on interest rates swaps and interest and dividend income (expense) net of transaction fees on total return swaps are similar to net investment income and are more appropriately classified as recurring.
Our calculation of Adjusted Net Investment Income may differ from the calculation of similarly titled non-GAAP financial measures by our peers, with the result that these non-GAAP financial measures might not be directly comparable. In addition, because Adjusted Net Investment Income is an incomplete measure of our financial results and differs from net investment income computed in accordance with U.S. GAAP, it should be considered supplementary to, and not as a substitute for, net investment income computed in accordance with U.S. GAAP.
In setting our distributions, our Board of Trustees considers our earnings, liquidity, financial condition, distribution requirements, and financial covenants, along with other factors that our Board of Trustees may deem relevant from time to time.
