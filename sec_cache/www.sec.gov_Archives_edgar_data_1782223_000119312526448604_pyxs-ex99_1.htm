Pyxis Oncology Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Highlights Progress Toward Pivotal Development of Micvotabart Pelidotin (MICVO)

 

Updated Phase 1 monotherapy results in second-line and beyond recurrent/metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (2L+ R/M HNSCC) showed compelling efficacy data with a manageable safety profile

 

FDA feedback on dose selection from the End-of-Phase 2 meeting expected in the first quarter of 2027; global Phase 3 HeadlinerSM trial initiation planned for mid-2027

 

Updated overall survival data from the MICVO Phase 1 monotherapy study expected in the first half of 2027

 

MICVO Phase 1 monotherapy data in previously treated R/M HNSCC to be presented in a late-breaking oral presentation and preclinical findings to be presented in two posters at the 38th EORTC-NCI-AACR Symposium

 

December 2026 corporate update to outline MICVO’s development strategy in R/M HNSCC, including updated data from the first-line pembrolizumab combination study

 

Quarter-end cash and net upfront proceeds from October public offering expected to fund operations into the first half of 2028

 

BOSTON, November 5, 2026 -- Pyxis Oncology, Inc. (Nasdaq: PYXS), a clinical-stage company developing next-generation therapeutics for difficult-to-treat cancers, today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, and highlighted continued progress in the development of micvotabart pelidotin (MICVO).

 

“The September monotherapy data reinforce our confidence in MICVO’s potential to serve a significant population of patients with previously treated recurrent/metastatic head and neck cancer,” said Tom Civik, Chief Executive Officer and Chairman of Pyxis Oncology. “We have aligned with feedback received from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and the European Medicines Agency (EMA) on the design of the global Phase 3 HeadlinerSM trial and plan to initiate it in mid-2027, with FDA feedback on dose selection expected in the first quarter of 2027. We look forward to presenting MICVO monotherapy data, along with two preclinical posters, at the EORTC-NCI-AACR Symposium in November, and providing a comprehensive update on our head and neck cancer strategy in December, including updated efficacy and safety data from MICVO in combination with pembrolizumab in the first-line setting. Our strengthened balance sheet positions us to execute on these priorities, continue evaluating MICVO’s broader potential and advance toward becoming a pre-commercial organization.”

 

Pipeline Updates

 

•
Pyxis Oncology has aligned with feedback from the FDA and the EMA on the design of the global pivotal Phase 3 HeadlinerSM trial, which the Company plans to initiate in mid-2027. The randomized, open-label, two-arm trial will evaluate MICVO monotherapy in patients with second-line and beyond recurrent/metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (2L+ R/M HNSCC) who have progressed following treatment with both platinum-based chemotherapy and an anti-PD-1 therapy. Approximately 500 patients are expected to be randomized 1:1 to receive MICVO or investigator’s choice of cetuximab, docetaxel or methotrexate. The co-primary endpoints are overall response rate and overall survival.

 

•
Pyxis Oncology expects to provide an update in the first quarter of 2027 on FDA feedback regarding dose selection following its End-of-Phase 2 meeting.

 

•
Updated overall survival data from the ongoing MICVO monotherapy study are expected in the first half of 2027.

 

•
Additional data from the Phase 1/2 combination study of MICVO and Merck’s anti-PD-1 therapy KEYTRUDA® (pembrolizumab) in first-line (1L) R/M HNSCC, including initial durability data to inform selection of the recommended Phase 3 dose, are expected in the second half of 2027.

 

 

Reported Updated Phase 1 Monotherapy Data in 2L+ R/M HNSCC

 

On September 9, 2026, Pyxis Oncology announced updated data from its ongoing global Phase 1 monotherapy study of MICVO in 2L+ R/M HNSCC, as of a data cutoff of August 18, 2026.

 

In the efficacy-evaluable population receiving MICVO at 5.4 mg/kg intravenously once every three weeks (IV Q3W) at or below a dose cap (N=33):

 

•
Confirmed objective response rate was 36% (12/33), and disease control rate was 94% (31/33).

 

•
Responses were rapid and deep, with 75% of responders responding by the first scan at six weeks and 83% of responders achieving greater than 50% tumor reduction.

 

•
Median progression-free survival was 6.2 months (95% CI: 4.8–8.8), and the 12-month overall survival probability was 79% (95% CI: 58.1, 90.3). Median overall survival had not yet been reached.

 

•
Clinical activity was observed across HPV status and prior-treatment subgroups.

 

In the safety-evaluable population (N=35), no new safety signals were observed. Dose capping reduced the frequency and severity of adverse events in high body weight patients.

About Micvotabart Pelidotin (MICVO)

 

Micvotabart pelidotin (MICVO, formerly PYX-201) is an investigational antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) that uniquely targets extradomain-B of fibronectin (EDB+FN), a non-cellular structural component of the tumor extracellular matrix. MICVO is designed to generate a multi-pronged attack on difficult-to-treat cancers by directly killing cancer cells, reducing extracellular matrix density, inhibiting tumor angiogenesis and mobilizing an anti-tumor immune response.

 

MICVO received Fast Track Designation from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration for the treatment of adult patients with R/M HNSCC whose disease has progressed following treatment with platinum-based chemotherapy and an anti-PD-(L)1 therapy.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

About Pyxis Oncology

 

Pyxis Oncology, Inc. is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing therapeutics for difficult-to-treat cancers. The Company’s lead candidate, micvotabart pelidotin (MICVO), is a first-in-concept antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) that targets extradomain-B of fibronectin (EDB+FN), a non-cellular structural component of the tumor extracellular matrix (ECM). EDB+FN is selectively overexpressed in the tumor microenvironment of a wide range of solid tumors and largely absent from normal adult tissues. MICVO is designed to treat solid tumors through a three-pronged mechanism of action: direct cancer cell killing, bystander effect and immunogenic cell death. MICVO is currently being evaluated as monotherapy in a Phase 1 clinical study in patients with recurrent/metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (R/M HNSCC) and in combination with Merck’s anti-PD-1 therapy, KEYTRUDA® (pembrolizumab) in a Phase 1/2 clinical study in patients with R/M HNSCC and other solid tumors. Pyxis Oncology is focused on advancing MICVO, with the goal of improving outcomes for patients living with R/M HNSCC and contributing to meaningful progress in cancer treatment.
