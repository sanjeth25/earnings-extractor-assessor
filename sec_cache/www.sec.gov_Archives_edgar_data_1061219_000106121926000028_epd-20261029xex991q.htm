Enterprise Reports Third Quarter 2026 Earnings
Houston, Texas (Thursday, October 29, 2026) – Enterprise Products Partners L.P. (“Enterprise”) (NYSE: EPD) today announced its financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.
Financial Highlights (3Q 2026 compared to 3Q 2025, as applicable)
•Net income attributable to common unitholders: $1.6 billion, $0.75 per diluted common unit, up 21%
•Adjusted EBITDA: $2.8 billion, up 16%
•Operational DCF: $2.3 billion, which provided 1.9x coverage of distributions declared for 3Q 2026, up 27%; retained $1.1 billion of DCF
•Adjusted CFFO: $2.4 billion, up 18%
•Common unit repurchases: $112 million for the quarter and $437 million for the 12 months ended September 30, 2026; 36% cumulative utilization of $5.0 billion buyback program
•For the 12 months ended September 30, 2026, the payout ratio, comprised of distributions to common unitholders and common unit buybacks, was 54% of Adjusted CFFO
•Distributions declared: $0.56 per common unit, or $2.24 per common unit annualized, up 2.8%

Capital Investment and Operational Highlights (3Q 2026 compared to 3Q 2025, as applicable)
•Record Permian natural gas processing inlet volumes: 4.6 Bcf/d, up 12% and record total natural gas processing inlet volumes: 8.5 Bcf/d, up 6%
•Record equivalent pipeline volumes: 14.8 MMBPD, up 7%
•Marine terminal volumes: 2.5 MMBPD, up 28%
•Capital investments for 3Q 2026: $1.1 billion, comprised of $0.9 billion for growth capital projects and $185 million for sustaining capital expenditures
•Growth capital spending for 2026, net of $600 million of proceeds from asset sales, is expected to be in the range of $2.9 to $3.4 billion; and $650 million for sustaining capital expenditures
Conference Call to Discuss Third Quarter 2026 Earnings
Enterprise will host a conference call today to discuss third quarter 2026 earnings. The call will be webcast live beginning at 9:00 a.m. CT and may be accessed by visiting the partnership’s website at www.enterpriseproducts.com.
Adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization (“Adjusted EBITDA”), distributable cash flow (“DCF”), Operational distributable cash flow (“Operational DCF”), Adjusted cash flow from operations (“Adjusted CFFO”), total gross operating margin, and adjusted free cash flow (“Adjusted FCF”) are non-generally accepted accounting principle (“non-GAAP”) financial measures that are defined and reconciled later in this press release.
As used in this press release, “NGL” means natural gas liquids, “LPG” means liquefied petroleum gas, “BPD” means barrels per day, “MBPD” means thousand barrels per day, “MMBPD” means million barrels per day, “MMcf/d” means million cubic feet per day, “Bcf/d” means billion cubic feet per day, “BBtus/d” means billion British thermal units per day, “TBtus/d” means trillion British thermal units per day, and “PDH” means propane dehydrogenation.


“Enterprise reported strong operating and financial performance for the third quarter of 2026,” said A.J. “Jim” Teague, co-chief executive officer of Enterprise’s general partner. “We handled record volumes at our Permian Basin natural gas processing plants, our NGL pipelines, natural gas pipelines and ethane marine export terminals. This strong performance was broad based with three of our segments reporting increases in gross operating margin compared to the third quarter of last year. Total gross operating margin for the third quarter of 2026 increased 15 percent to $2.7 billion. Operational distributable cash flow for the third quarter of 2026 increased 27 percent to $2.3 billion, which provided 1.9 times coverage of the partnership’s distribution declared for the quarter and we retained $1.1 billion of distributable cash flow to reinvest in the growth of the partnership, fund buybacks of partnership units and reduce debt.”
“We continue to see strong natural gas and NGL production growth from higher natural gas to oil production ratios in the Permian Basin that flows through our integrated NGL system to serve both domestic and international end use markets. Economic growth and disruption caused by global geopolitical conflicts continue to drive strong international demand across our assets for U.S. produced NGLs, crude oil, petrochemicals, refined products and motor gasoline additives. The partnership also benefited from new assets that began operations over the past twelve months including our eleventh natural gas processing plant in the Delaware Basin, the Bahia NGL pipeline, Frac 14 at our Mont Belvieu complex and the second phase of our Neches River NGL marine terminal,” stated Teague.
“Enterprise currently has $6.3 billion of growth capital projects under construction that will begin commercial operations between now and 2028. In the fourth quarter of 2026, the next projects scheduled for completion are the fourth expansion of our LPG marine terminal on the Houston Ship Channel and our new Athena natural gas processing plant in the Midland Basin. The LPG marine terminal expansion will increase the partnership’s LPG loading rate by approximately 300 MBPD to a total of 1.1 MMBPD, the largest LPG export facility in the world. The Athena plant will have the capacity to process 300 MMcf/d of natural gas and extract up to 40 MBPD of NGLs. These projects as well as our projects under construction provide visibility to Enterprise’s future growth in cash flow per unit and the value of our partnership,” Teague concluded.
Review of Third Quarter 2026 Results
Total gross operating margin for the third quarter of 2026 increased $350 million to $2.7 billion compared to the third quarter of 2025. This includes a $21 million increase in unrealized mark-to-market (“MTM”) earnings on financial instruments used in our hedging activities.
NGL Pipelines & Services – Gross operating margin from the NGL Pipelines & Services segment was $1.5 billion for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $1.3 billion for the third quarter of 2025.
Gross operating margin from the natural gas processing business and related NGL marketing activities was $488 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $354 million for the third quarter of 2025. Natural gas processing plant inlet volumes were a record 8.5 Bcf/d in the third quarter of 2026, a 6 percent increase compared to the third quarter of 2025. Notably, inlet volumes to the partnership’s natural gas processing plants in the Permian Basin increased 12 percent to a record 4.6 Bcf/d for the third quarter of 2026. Total fee-based natural gas processing volumes increased 742 MMcf/d to a record 8.2 Bcf/d in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of last year. Total equity NGL-equivalent production volumes increased 8 percent to 242 MBPD in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025. The following summarizes the gross operating margin variance for this business, with results for the third quarter of 2026 as compared to the third quarter of 2025:
•NGL marketing gross operating margin increased $75 million primarily due to higher average sales margins, primarily driven from marketing strategies associated with our export and natural gas processing assets.
•Delaware Basin natural gas processing facilities reported a $31 million increase in gross operating margin primarily due to a 359 MMcf/d increase in fee-based natural gas processing volumes and a 6 MBPD increase in equity NGL-equivalent production volumes.
•Midland Basin natural gas processing facilities reported a $12 million increase in gross operating margin primarily due to higher volumes and lower operating costs, partially offset by lower average processing margins. Fee-based natural gas processing volumes increased 280 MMcf/d and equity NGL-equivalent production volumes increased 13 MBPD.

Gross operating margin from the NGL pipelines and storage business was $785 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $746 million for the third quarter of 2025. Total NGL pipeline volumes were a record 5.0 MMBPD in the third quarter of 2026, a 284 MBPD, or 6 percent, increase over the third quarter of 2025. Total NGL marine terminal volumes were 1.2 MMBPD in the third quarter of 2026, a 288 MBPD increase compared to the third quarter of 2025. The following summarizes the gross operating margin variance for this business, with results for the third quarter of 2026 as compared to the third quarter of 2025:
•Gross operating margin from the Morgan's Point, Neches River and Enterprise Hydrocarbons Terminals and the associated Channel pipeline increased $21 million primarily driven by higher export volumes following the completion of the second phase of the Neches River Terminal in May 2026, partially offset by higher operating costs. On a combined basis, ethane export volumes increased 139 MBPD and LPG export volumes increased 149 MBPD.
Gross operating margin from the NGL fractionation business was $262 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $203 million for the third quarter of 2025. Total NGL fractionation volumes were 1.9 MMBPD for the third quarter of 2026 compared to 1.6 MMBPD for the third quarter of 2025. The higher gross operating margin and fractionation volumes were primarily due to Frac 14, which was placed into service in the fourth quarter of 2025.
Crude Oil Pipelines & Services – Gross operating margin from the Crude Oil Pipelines & Services segment was $367 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $371 million for the third quarter of 2025. Total crude oil pipeline volumes were 2.7 MMBPD in the third quarter of 2026, a 48 MBPD increase compared to the third quarter of 2025. Total crude oil marine terminal volumes were 926 MBPD in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 720 MBPD in the third quarter of 2025. The following summarizes the gross operating margin variance for this segment, with results for the third quarter of 2026 as compared to the third quarter of 2025:
•On a combined basis, gross operating margin from our Texas crude oil pipelines, related terminals and marketing activities (excluding the Seaway Pipeline) decreased $14 million primarily due to higher operating costs.
•Gross operating margin from crude oil activities at the Enterprise Hydrocarbons Terminal (“EHT”) increased $6 million primarily due to higher loading and other revenues. Crude oil marine terminal volumes at EHT increased 163 MBPD.
Natural Gas Pipelines & Services – Gross operating margin for the Natural Gas Pipelines & Services segment was $411 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $339 million for the third quarter of 2025. Total natural gas pipeline volumes were a record 22.7 TBtus/d in the third quarter of 2026, an 8 percent increase compared to 21.0 TBtus/d for the same quarter in 2025. The following summarizes the gross operating margin variance for this segment, with results for the third quarter of 2026 as compared to the third quarter of 2025:
•Gross operating margin from natural gas marketing activities increased $31 million primarily due to higher average sales margins and higher MTM earnings.
•Gross operating margin from our Delaware Basin Gathering System increased $17 million primarily due to higher treating and other revenues, higher average gathering fees, and a 423 BBtus/d increase in natural gas gathering volumes, which were partially offset by higher operating costs.
Petrochemical & Refined Products Services – Gross operating margin for the Petrochemical & Refined Products Services segment was $430 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $370 million for the third quarter of 2025. Total segment pipeline volumes were 1.2 MMBPD in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 1.1 MMBPD in the third quarter of 2025. Total marine terminal volumes were 413 MBPD in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 347 MBPD for the third quarter of 2025. The following summarizes the gross operating margin variance for this segment, with results for the third quarter of 2026 as compared to the third quarter of 2025:
•Gross operating margin from our octane enhancement and related plant operations increased $19 million primarily due to higher sales volumes attributable to strong international demand for octane additives for motor gasoline.
•Gross operating margin from refined products marketing activities increased $10 million primarily due to higher sales volumes.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
This press release and accompanying schedules include the non-GAAP financial measures of total gross operating margin, Adjusted CFFO, Adjusted FCF, DCF, Operational DCF and Adjusted EBITDA. The accompanying schedules provide definitions of these non-GAAP financial measures and reconciliations to their most directly comparable financial measure calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP. Our non-GAAP financial measures should not be considered as alternatives to GAAP measures such as net income, operating income, net cash flow provided by operating activities or any other measure of financial performance calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP. Our non-GAAP financial measures may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies because they may not calculate such measures in the same manner as we do.
Company Information and Use of Forward-Looking Statements
Enterprise Products Partners L.P. is one of the largest publicly traded partnerships and a leading North American provider of midstream energy services to producers and consumers of natural gas, NGLs, crude oil, refined products and petrochemicals. Services include: natural gas gathering, treating, processing, transportation and storage; NGL transportation, fractionation, storage and marine terminals; crude oil gathering, transportation, storage and marine terminals; petrochemical and refined products transportation, storage and marine terminals; and a marine transportation business that operates on key U.S. inland and intracoastal waterway systems. The partnership’s assets currently include more than 50,000 miles of pipelines; over 300 million barrels of storage capacity for NGLs, crude oil, petrochemicals and refined products; and 14 billion cubic feet of natural gas storage capacity.

Adjusted CFFO is a non-GAAP measure that represents net cash flow provided by operating activities before the net effect of changes in operating accounts. We believe that it is important to consider this non-GAAP measure as it can often be a better way to measure the amount of cash generated from our operations that can be used to fund our capital investments or return value to our investors through cash distributions and buybacks, without regard for fluctuations caused by timing of when amounts earned or incurred were collected, received or paid from period to period.
Adjusted FCF is a non-GAAP measure that reflects cash generated from our operations, excluding the net effect of changes in operating accounts, after accounting for capital expenditures. We believe that Adjusted FCF is important to traditional investors since it reflects the amount of cash available for reducing debt, investing in additional capital projects and/or paying distributions, without regard for fluctuations caused by timing of when amounts earned or incurred were collected, received or paid from period to period. Since we partner with other companies to fund certain capital projects of our consolidated subsidiaries, our determination of Adjusted FCF appropriately reflects the amount of cash contributed from and distributed to noncontrolling interests.

DCF is an important non-GAAP liquidity measure for our common unitholders since it serves as an indicator of our success in providing a cash return on investment. Specifically, this liquidity measure indicates to investors whether or not we are generating cash flows at a level that can sustain or support an increase in our quarterly cash distributions. DCF is also a quantitative standard used by the investment community with respect to publicly traded partnerships because the value of a partnership unit is, in part, measured by its yield, which is based on the amount of cash distributions a partnership can pay to a common unitholder.
Operational DCF, which is defined as DCF excluding the impact of proceeds from asset sales and other matters and monetization of interest rate derivative instruments, is a supplemental non-GAAP liquidity measure that quantifies the portion of cash available for distribution to common unitholders that was generated from our normal operations. We believe that it is important to consider this non-GAAP measure as it provides an enhanced perspective of our assets’ ability to generate cash flows without regard for certain items that do not reflect our core operations.
