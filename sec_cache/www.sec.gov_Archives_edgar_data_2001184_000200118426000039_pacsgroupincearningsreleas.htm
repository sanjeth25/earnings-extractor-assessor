PACS Group, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

Conference Call and Webcast Scheduled for Tomorrow, November 4, 2026, at 11:30 am ET.

SALT LAKE CITY, Utah, November 3, 2026 – PACS Group, Inc. (NYSE: PACS) (“PACS” or the “Company”), which together with its subsidiaries is one of the largest post-acute healthcare companies in the United States, announced operating results for the third quarter of 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

•Revenue was $1.50 billion, an increase of 11.5% over prior year.
•Net income was $60.8 million, an increase of $8.5 million, or 16.2% from $52.3 million in the prior-year period.
•Diluted Earnings Per Share was $0.37, an increase of 15.6% over prior year, and Adjusted Earnings Per Share was $0.63, an increase of 37.0% over prior year.1
•Adjusted EBITDA was $175.7 million, an increase of $44.2 million, or 33.6% from $131.5 million in the prior-year period.1
•Adjusted EBITDAR was $273.9 million.1

Third Quarter 2026 Select KPIs

•On a same-store basis, which includes the 284 skilled nursing facilities (“SNFs”) operated by the Company as of the beginning of 2025, SNF revenue increased 6.4% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the prior-year period. Occupancy improved to 91.3% from 89.3% in the third quarter of 2025, and skilled mix increased in both revenue and nursing patient days.
•The Company had 250 facilities, or 78.9%, of its skilled nursing portfolio achieve a 4 or 5 star CMS Quality Measure Star rating, with its 196 mature facilities achieving an average rating of 4.5 as of September 30, 2026.
•Overall occupancy was 90.2%, compared to an industry average of 80.2%. Mature facilities occupancy was 93.1%.
•Mature facilities skilled mix was 31.3%, while overall skilled mix increased to 29.1%, an improvement of 90 basis points from 28.2% in the prior-year period, driven by continued improvement in our Ramping facilities cohort.
•Cash provided by operating activities was $583.1 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.
•The Company deployed $213.0 million in capital for M&A during the third quarter of 2026, bringing the total M&A investment to $403.8 million for the first nine months of the year.
•As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $707.4 million in available liquidity, including $115.3 million of cash and cash equivalents.

“Our third quarter results reflect the continued progress our teams are making across the organization, both in the quality of care we provide and the performance of our facilities,” said Jason Murray, PACS Chief Executive Officer. “Same-store revenue increased 6.4%, occupancy improved to 91.3%, and skilled mix continued to strengthen. Importantly, 78.9% of our skilled nursing facilities achieved four- or five-star CMS Quality Measure ratings, reflecting the dedication of our local teams and their commitment to delivering exceptional patient care.

“At the same time, we're entering an exciting period of growth for PACS. Our recent expansion into Florida and continued growth in Texas significantly broaden our geographic reach and create new opportunities to bring our operating model to more communities. As we continue to grow, our focus remains on empowering local leaders, improving clinical outcomes, and building strong operations that create lasting value for our patients, employees, and shareholders.”

“Our third quarter results demonstrate the increasing earnings power of the PACS operating model, with Adjusted EBITDA growth significantly outpacing revenue growth,” said Carey Hendrickson, PACS Chief Financial Officer. “Revenue increased 11.5% year-over-year to $1.50 billion, while Adjusted EBITDA grew 33.6% to $175.7 million. Growth Highlights
Subsidiaries of PACS entered into a definitive agreement to acquire the operations of 10 skilled nursing facilities and one assisted living facility in Texas. As of November 1, 2026, PACS has closed on its acquisition of the operations of all 11 Texas facilities.

Additionally, as previously announced on September 1, 2026, subsidiaries of PACS entered into a definitive agreement to acquire the operations of 32 skilled nursing facilities across Florida, marking its entry into a new state. Collectively, the facilities comprise 4,049 skilled nursing beds. On October 1, 2026, PACS closed its acquisition of the operations of all 32 Florida facilities.

Revised 2026 Business Outlook

“Given the continued excellent performance of our portfolio across all cohorts, we are increasing our full-year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance to a range of $685 million to $695 million, up from our prior range of $640 million to $660 million,” said Hendrickson. “At the midpoint, this represents approximately 37% growth over 2025.”

“We are also increasing our revenue guidance to $5.95 billion to $6.05 billion, up from our prior range of $5.75 billion to $5.85 billion."

“Our guidance reflects approximately $5 million of EBITDA contribution from retroactive quality incentive payments in Ohio received in the month of October. We also continue to see a robust pipeline of acquisition opportunities and remain actively engaged in evaluating transactions that meet our strategic, operational and financial criteria,” said Hendrickson.
As of today, PACS's growing portfolio comprises 398 healthcare operations across 21 states. PACS owns 66 facilities and leases an additional 49 facilities with partial ownership in real estate. PACS holds 37 purchase options on leased facilities and 20 purchase options through partnerships. The Company remains focused on acquiring underperforming and moderately performing operations where its operating model can drive meaningful improvement, while selectively investing in real estate to support long-term value creation.

About PACS™
PACS Group, Inc. is a holding company investing in post-acute healthcare facilities, professionals, and ancillary services. Founded in 2013, PACS Group is one of the largest post-acute platforms in the United States. Its independent subsidiaries operate 398 post-acute care facilities across 21 states serving over 38,900 patients daily. Non-GAAP Financial Measures
In addition to our results provided throughout that are determined in accordance with GAAP, we also present the following non-GAAP financial measures: Adjusted Net Income, Adjusted Earnings Per Share, EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDAR (collectively, Non-GAAP Financial Measures). Adjusted Net Income, Adjusted Earnings Per Share, EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA are performance measures. Adjusted EBITDAR is a valuation measure. These Non-GAAP Financial Measures have no standardized meaning defined by GAAP, and therefore have limitations as analytical tools, and they should not be considered in isolation, or as a substitute for analysis of our results as reported in accordance with GAAP. Performance Measures
We use Adjusted Net Income, Adjusted Earnings Per Share, EBITDA, and Adjusted EBITDA to facilitate internal comparisons of our historical operating performance on a more consistent basis, as well as for business planning and forecasting purposes. In addition, we believe the presentation of these measures is useful to investors, analysts and other interested parties in comparing our operating performance across reporting periods on a consistent basis by excluding items that we do not believe are indicative of our ongoing operating performance.
Adjusted Net Income – We calculate Adjusted Net Income as net income, adjusted for net loss attributable to noncontrolling interest, further adjusted for non-core business items as listed in Adjusted EBITDA, as well as the related income tax effects of these adjustments.
Adjusted Earnings Per Share – We calculate Adjusted Earnings Per Share by dividing Adjusted Net Income by the weighted‑average diluted shares outstanding for the applicable period.
EBITDA – We calculate EBITDA as net income, adjusted for net loss attributable to noncontrolling interest, before: interest expense; provision for income taxes; and depreciation and amortization.
Adjusted EBITDA – We calculate Adjusted EBITDA as EBITDA further adjusted for non-core business items, which for the reported periods includes, to the extent applicable, costs incurred to acquire operations that are not capitalizable, stock-based compensation expense, legal and other costs, and certain one-time expenses that are not representative of our underlying operating performance. Costs related to acquisitions include costs related to our acquisition of operations, including related costs such as legal fees, financial and tax due diligence, consulting and escrow fees. Legal and other costs include legal and professional fees incurred associated with the Audit Committee’s independent investigation during the years ended December 31, 2025 and 2024, and with other ongoing investigations and SEC matters.
Valuation Measure
We use Adjusted EBITDAR as a measure to determine the value of prospective acquisitions and to assess the enterprise value of our business without regard to differences in capital structures and leasing arrangements. In addition, we believe that Adjusted EBITDAR is also a commonly used measure by investors, analysts and other interested parties to compare the enterprise value of different companies in the healthcare industry without regard to differences in capital structures and leasing arrangements, particularly for companies with operating and finance leases. For example, finance lease expenditures are recorded in depreciation and interest and are therefore removed from Adjusted EBITDA, whereas operating lease expenditures are recorded in rent expense and are therefore retained in Adjusted EBITDA. Adjusted EBITDAR is a financial valuation measure that is not specified in GAAP, and is not displayed as a performance measure as it excludes rent expense, which is a normal and recurring cash operating expense, and is therefore presented only for the current period. While we believe that Adjusted EBITDAR provides useful insight regarding our underlying operations, excluding the impact of our operating leases, we must still incur cash operating expenses related to our operating leases and rent and such expenses are necessary to operate our leased operations. As a result, Adjusted EBITDAR may understate the extent of our cash operating expenses for the respective period relative to our cash needs to operate our leased operations and business.

Adjusted EBITDAR – We calculate Adjusted EBITDAR as Adjusted EBITDA plus rent-cost of services.
